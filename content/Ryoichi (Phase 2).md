### Traits

***Dream Omniscience.*** Ryoichi knows exactly where all creatures are.

***Legendary Resistance (1/day).*** If the creature fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Split Form.*** Ryoichi has 2 bodies. Each has its own Hit Points. When both are killed, Ryoichi advances to the next phase: [[Ryoichi (Phase 3)]].

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Ryoichi makes two Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with an Onibi's Blast or Fiendish Lies.

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* `dice: 1d20 +7` (+7) to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* `dice: 1d8 +4` (1d8 + 4) slashing damage. (Avg. 8)

***Onibi's Blast.*** *Ranged Spell Attack Roll:* `dice: 1d20+6` (+6) to hit, range 30/60 ft., single target 
- *Hit:* `dice: 1d8 +4` (1d8 + 4) fire damage. (Avg. 8)
	- Target is [[Burning]].

***Fiendish Lies (Target TUDEK, unusable once successful).*** Target must succeed a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or become [[Charmed]] to believe all it's allies to be foes and all it's foes to be allies. Target may reroll saving throw at the end of each of its turns or whenever it takes damage, ending the effect on a success.
- upon attempt, ryoichi tells tudek one of these lies:
	- {party member} thinks your mudbrew sucks
	- {party member} wants to take your egg
	- {party member} thinks Not Dead dying was your fault

### Reactions

***Onibi's Wrath.*** Ryoichi can cast [[Hellish Rebuke]] at 1st level.

### Legendary Actions

*Legendary Action Uses: 2. Immediately after another creature's turn, the creature can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The creature regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*

- [-] ***Onibi's Blast.*** Ryoichi makes an Onibi's Blast attack.

- [ ] ***Flame Step.*** The oni casts [[Misty Step]] on itself, bathing itself in blue flames and smoke. The creature can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
