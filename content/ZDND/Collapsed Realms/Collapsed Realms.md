The known universe was once Nine Realms, separate yet inextricably linked by the Realm Between Realms and the Yggdrasil, the World Tree, that held them aloft in the cosmos.

Under the rule of the Aesir Gods, the Realms saw both prosperity and grand conflict in the millennia since the creation of the universe, yet a prophesized fall of the Aesir and their home realm, Asgard, loomed over the realms.

Despite great effort, Odin the Allfather, leader of the Aesir, would be incapable of stopping the apocalypse known as Ragnarök. 

### Ragnarök and the Collapse
The prophesized destruction of Asgard, known as Ragnarök, lit a cosmic fire in the Realm Between Realms. A fire that would consume Yggdrasil and burn away the veil that separated the realms. One by one, the Realms collapsed into each other, the effects of which were apocalyptic to the mortal societies of the Realms. Over a billion lives were lost as Realms were ripped apart, and reconstructed by cosmic forces.

After the Collapse, survivors were few, crawling through the ruins of the Old World trying to reestablish a footing in a hostile, unfamiliar world. But Mortal's lives are short and their memories shorter, as they persevere and build a new home in the remnants of their old one.

## The Nine Realms
The Nine primary Realms were the known lands before Ragnarok and the Collapse. Other planes of existence are theorized to exist, based on evidence gathered by scholars, however it is unclear what these are and if they are new after the Collapse, were always there and inaccessible or if they are of a different nature.

Regardless, the primary Nine Realms are as follows:

### Asgard
Home of the Aesir Gods, Asgard was the smallest of all realms housing a small population of mortals that served under the Allfather as well as refugees from various crisis.

#### Asgard's Destruction
The key event of Ragnarök and the prophesized fall of the Aesir saw a great battle of forces that would result in Asgard's destruction.

### Alfheim
Home of the Fae and Grae Elves, Alfheim was characterized as two sides of a whole. The Fae was a chaotic wildland filled with wild magic. The Grae was a desert atop a great network of caves. Each centered around the powerful, sacred Light of Alfheim. A flow of spiritual energy, that stretched into the sky like a beacon.

#### Post-Collapse
After the Collapse of the Realms, Alfheim's lands would be melded with Midgard alongside many others. The structure that contained and focused the Light of Alfheim would be destroyed, diffusing it within the cosmos.

### Hel
Hel, sometimes referred to as Helheim, is the frozen wasteland that houses the souls of the dead. Hel's gates are watched over by Hela, a giant eagle who flaps her wings to produce the powerful Winds of Hel to ensure no living being enters the afterlife. Only the races of Men, Aesir and Vanir arrive in Hel upon their death, although it is unclear why. All others are returned to the Light of Alfheim.

It is believed that should you have lived a good life, you will feel the warmth of your loved ones in Hel, rather than the chill of the wind, until the day you fall again and are eaten by Nidhogg, and returned to the Light.

#### Post-Collapse
Hel is missing in the Post-Collapse era. Though a living soul could never walk past Hel's gates, the Realm still could be travelled to before Ragnarök, however the Collapse seems to have severed both physical and interplanar connections to Hel.

### Jotunheim
Home of the Jotuns, otherwise known as Giants, Jotunheim was one continuous and expansive Mountain range with other biomes nested in the valleys between. Jotunheim was the unfortunate battleground between the Aesir and True Giants, leaving the realm scattered in the remains of the fallen giants.

#### Post-Collapse
Jotunheim's endless mountains would meld with Midgard's during the Collapse creating the many great mountain ranges of the Collapsed Realms.

### Midgard
The largest of the realms and native home to the race of Men as well as the chosen home of many others, in addition to being a refuge for Realm travelers. Being a kind of nexus of realms, Midgard had many kinds of biomes, climates and geologies. It was the setting for many of the great events in the history of the Realms.

#### Post-Collapse
Being at the center of Yggdrasil, Midgard was the unfortunate center of the Collapse in which the other Realms drifted into.

### Muspelheim
The Realm of Primordial Fire, Muspelheim is home to the Fire Giant Surtur and his infernal and demonic forces of chaos. Consisting of magma, basalt and brimstone, this realm was inhospitable to most races.

#### Post-Collapse
Muspelheim is believed to have become the bedrock upon which the rest of the Collapsed Realms sit atop. Its fires buried deep within the earth, below the caves known as the Underdark. Sightings of Infernals and Demons lead some to believe there are passageways for which one could walk from the surface to Muspelheim's warm embrace with enough determination.

### Svartalfheim
Realm of the Dvergar, Svartalfheim was a massive circular mountain range with bogs, sulfur springs and wetlands in the center lowlands. Dwarves inhabited the mountains, Gnomes in the foothills and Orcs in the lowlands. Svartalfheim was rich with resources and safe from a lot of outside influence other than the Gods.

#### Post-Collapse
The mountains of Svartalfheim are dwarfed by those of Jotunheim in the Collapsed Realms, however the bogs and sulfur springs that can be found betray that it too fell into one with all the others.

### Niflheim
The Realm of Primordial Ice, Niflheim was the first of the two Primordial Realms during the dawn of the cosmos. It is believed that should there be evidence of existence pre-dating Ginnungagap, it is forever and impossibly frozen in Niflheim's ice.

#### Post-Collapse
Despite the cold that can be found in the Collapsed Realms, no land is known to bear the sheer chill that Niflheim was known for. It is believed that like Hel, it is lost somewhere in the cosmos.

### Vanaheim
Home of the Vanir Gods, Vanaheim was a vast realm of dense and lush forests, jungles and savannahs. The Vanir, adept with controlling magic at a base level achieved many things and were envied by the Allfather. He would come to wage war upon them, and upon winning, adopt many Vanir into is own family and realm.

The remaining Vanir, protested this assimilation, and would go into hiding. Their latent magics in the ruins of their civilization would give birth to Beastfolk.

#### Post-Collapse
Vanaheim would too suffer the effects of the Collapse with its lands being spread far and wide in the new Collapsed Realms. Surprisingly, many of the Vanir ruins that once dotted Vanaheim's landscape survived the calamity.
