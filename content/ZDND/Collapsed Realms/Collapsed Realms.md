The known universe was once 9 realms, separate yet inextricably linked by the Realm Between Realms and the Yggdrasil, the World Tree, that held them aloft in the cosmos.

### The Nine Realms
#### Asgard
Home of the Aesir Gods, Asgard was the smallest of all realms housing a small population of mortals that served under the Allfather as well as refugees from various crisis.

#### Alfheim
Home of the Fae and Grae Elves, Alfheim was characterized as two sides of a whole. The Fae was a chaotic wildland filled with wild magic. The Grae was a desert atop a great network of caves. Each centered around the powerful, sacred Light of Alfheim. A flow of spiritual energy, that stretched into the sky like a beacon.

#### Jotunheim
Home of the Jotuns, otherwise known as Giants, Jotunheim was one continuous and expansive Mountain range with other biomes nested in the valleys between. Jotunheim was the unfortunate battleground between the Aesir and True Giants, leaving the realm scattered in the remains of the fallen giants.

### Ragnarök and the Collapse
The destruction of Asgard, lit a cosmic fire in the Realm Between Realms. A fire that would consume Yggdrasil and burn away the veil that separated the realms. One by one, the Realms collapsed into each other, the effects of which were apocalyptic to the mortal societies of the Realms. Over a billion lives were lost as Realms were ripped apart, and reconstructed by cosmic forces.
