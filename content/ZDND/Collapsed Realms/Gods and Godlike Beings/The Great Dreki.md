The Great Dreki are the three great scaled beasts of old: Jörmungandr the World Serpent, Fafnir the First Dragon, and Nidhogg the Matriarch of Malice.

Although not truly gods, the Great Dreki are worshipped like Deities by the lesser Dreki of the Collapsed Realms. Dreki is a term that includes any scaled, sapient life such as Dragons, Serpents, Dragonborn, Kobolds, and Lizardfolk. By definition, Turtlefolk should be included in this cultural identifier, yet they are often excluded by their scaled brethren, which most don't really seem to mind.

#### Before Ragnarök
It is believed the three Great Dreki were aware of their status and perceived relation in their time, and perhaps even interacted to some degree, however any evidence of this was muddied with the Collapse.

### Jörmungandr
Son of Loki and his giantess wife Angrboda, it is unknown why Jörmungandr was a serpent. Some believe he took the same form as his shapeshifting parents assumed during his conception. Others believe it to be a curse. Regardless, as a serpent, the Allfather cast him into the seas of Midgard where he grew to immense size and would become known as the World Serpent.

He notoriously had a cataclysmic rivalry with Thor, which would be one of the key moments during Ragnarök, their battle allegedly resulting in both of their deaths.

#### Post-Collapse
Jörmungandr is worshipped by Dreki as an icon of cycles of both nature and mortals, as well as vengeance, and honor. They pray to his memory and some believe that Jörmungandr will be reborn one day to live another life.

### Fafnir
Once a dwarf, Fafnir's father Dwarf King Hriedmarr was paid recompense a hoard of treasure by the Aesir for the accidental killing of Fafnir's brother Otr by Loki. Included in the hoard was the cursed ring of Andvari known as Andvaranaut. Despite warnings of the curse by Loki, Hriedmarr elected to keep the ring.

The curse caused Fafnir to become overwhelmed with greed. He killed his father and took the hoard and ring for himself. The curse would then cause Fafnir to forever be transformed into a winged wyrm, or the first dragon.

Fafnir's brother Regin would go through great lengths to imprison Fafnir, while he searched for a way to cure him of the curse. Fafnir would remain trapped until shortly before Ragnarök. It is unknown how he was freed.

#### Post-Collapse
Fafnir, having survived Ragnarök, is believed to have embarked on a personal quest of some kind, presumably to find a way to satisfy his greed and lust for power. 

His progeny of Dragons, Dragonborn and Kobolds alike, worship him and the grand power he represents.

### Nidhogg
Nidhogg, is the lindwyrm that once fed on the roots of Yggdrasil. She was a force of entropy, feeding directly on the cosmos as well as the dead in Hel, a final step in the journey of life for Men and Gods. Her title of "Matriarch of Malice" is a misnomer derived from the fear of her cosmic influence. She was believed to be caring for a clutch of eggs or young prior to Ragnarök, earning her Matriarchal title.

#### Post-Collapse
Ever since the Collapse, both Nidhogg and her young have gone missing. Some believe them dead, others believe the lindwyrms to still be feeding on what's left of the tree, sure to one day bring the missing realms collapsing into the others.

To Dreki, the Matriarch of Malice paradoxically represents both life and birth as well as death and destruction.
