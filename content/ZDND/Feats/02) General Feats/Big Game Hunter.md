*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Bigger They Are.** You have a +1 to attack rolls and +2 to damage rolls for each size category larger your target is than you.

**Harder They Fall.** When you force a target to make a saving throw, your effect's DC has a +1 bonus for each size category larger your target is than you.
