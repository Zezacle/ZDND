*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength, Dexterity or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**One Man Army.** When you are being flanked by hostile creatures, each time you are targeted by a melee attack, but the attack roll does not exceed your [[Armor Class|AC]], you can make a free [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attack]] against your attacker.
