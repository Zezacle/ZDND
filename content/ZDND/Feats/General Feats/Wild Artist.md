*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Camouflaged.** With 10 minutes of work, you can dress yourself up in a camouflage that matches nearby natural surroundings using soil, paint, foliage and other debris. While you are camouflaged, you have [[Advantage]] on [[Stealth]] checks and you can take the [[Hide]] action as a bonus action to blend in to your surroundings. Any creature that fails to detect you initially will be unable to spot you without magical means, even if you are in their line of sight as long as you remain motionless. Creatures have [[Disadvantage]] on [[Perception]] checks to detect you while you are camouflaged.

Your camouflage has a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to your character level + your proficiency bonus and takes damage when you take damage. The camouflage has [[Immunity]] to poison and psychic damage. When the camouflage's hit points reaches 0, it is destroyed and you no longer benefit from the effects and can't camouflage yourself again until you complete a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

**Cunning Artisan.** As part of a [[Short Rest]], you can harvest bone and hide from a slain beast, construct, dragon, monstrosity, or plant creature of size Small or larger to create one of the following items: a [[Arm Shield]], a [[Club]], a [[Javelin]], a [[Throwing Knife]], or 1d4 [[Blowpipe]] needles. To use this trait, you need a blade, such as a [[Dagger]], or appropriate [[Artisan's Tools]], such as [[Leatherworker's Tools]].
