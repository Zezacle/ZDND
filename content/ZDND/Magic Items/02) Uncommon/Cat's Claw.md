*Weapon (Dagger, Shortsword, Rapier), Uncommon*

*A blade adorned with a cat's head for the pommel. The blade retracts into the hilt when not in use. It seems to attract cats...*

You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

While carrying this magic weapon on your person, you have Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made in relation to feline creatures.
