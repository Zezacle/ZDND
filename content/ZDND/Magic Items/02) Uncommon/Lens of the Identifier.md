**Charges Left:** Recharges at Dawn

*A small monocle-like glass lens with a brass border. Looking through it reveals the arcane, but the focus length makes anything further than a few feet away very blurry.*

When looking through this lens, the viewer has the effects of the [[Detect Magic]] spell, but only up to 5 ft.

The viewer of the lens can use a Magic Action to expend a charge and cast [[Identify]] without using spell slots or needing components.
