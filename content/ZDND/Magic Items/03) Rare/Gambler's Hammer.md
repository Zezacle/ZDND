*Weapon (Light Hammer, Warhammer), Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Attunement:** Required

*A sturdy hammer with a symbol of luck carved in it's striking face. The pommel is shaped like an icosahedron.*

As a Bonus Action, roll a d20 to channel the Hammer's magic. For the next 30 seconds (5 rounds) all damage taken by the wielder and dealt by the hammer is multiplied by the resulting payout. Once activated, the effect cannot be deactivated or reactivated until the previous effect ends.

| d20 | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 | x0.0 |
| 5-8 | x0.5 |
| 9-12 | x1.0 |
| 13-16 | x1.5 |
| 14-20 | x2.0 |
