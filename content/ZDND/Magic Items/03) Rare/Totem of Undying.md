#### [[Totem of Undying]]
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires attunement)*

**Attunement:** Required

*A palm-sized golden totem with an emerald at its heart. It seems to hum with powerful abjuration magic.*

While the bearer is attuned to and within 5ft of the totem, they are under the effects of the [[Death Ward]] spell. The totem breaks when the spell is triggered.
