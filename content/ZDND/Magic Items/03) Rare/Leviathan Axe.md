*Weapon (Battleaxe), Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Mastery:** [[Topple]] 
**Properties:** [[Versatile]]
- One-Handed
	- 1d8 slashing
	- [[Vicious]]
- [[Two-Handed]]
	- 1d10 slashing
	- [[Siege]]
	- [[Vicious]]

**Attunement:** Required

*This finely-crafted, single-bladed battleaxe has a curved wood haft and ice-like engravings in its permanently frosted blade. A serpent's head is carved into the wooden pommel. All of the metalworking is cold to the touch.*

While wielding the Leviathan Axe, you can cast [[Ray of Frost]] as an [[Attack]] action, counting as one of your attacks for the purposes of Extra Attack, adding your Proficiency Bonus to the Attack Roll.

As a Bonus Action, the wielder can summon the Leviathan Axe to their hand from up to 100 feet away. The axe will fly through the air, bouncing off obstacles and travelling any indirect path to the owner, but will stop if no path is available at all (e.g. Locked in a room or container). Any creature caught in the path of the axe must make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 Slashing damage.

The Leviathan Axe returns to the wielder at the start of their next turn.
