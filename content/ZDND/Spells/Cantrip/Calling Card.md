*Transmutation Cantrip (Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Special

When cast upon a creature killed by you within 1 minute of its death, [[Calling Card]] reshapes the fatal wound in a way that unmistakably identifies you as the killer. You select the form this mark will take when you learn the spell, and you can’t change it later except through exceptional magic such as a [[Wish]] spell. No two creatures can share the same [[Calling Card]].
