*Level 1 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)*

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a prayer scroll)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

A shimmering field surrounds a creature of your choice within range, granting it a +2 bonus to [[Evasion Class|EC]] and [[Armor Class|AC]] for the duration.
