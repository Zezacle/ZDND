When you take the [[Feint Attack]] bonus action, make a Sleight of Hand check against a target within melee range.  The target must succeed on a Wisdom or Dexterity saving throw or the next attack roll against them has [[Advantage]] until they take damage or the start of their next turn.

If the weapon you are wielding when you take this bonus action has the [[Finesse]] property, you gain a +2 to your ability check.
