### Meshing With the Campaign and Party

#### Campaign Appropriate
The Campaign you are participating in may have restrictions or special classes, so reference the associated Campaign Brief. Additionally, think about the type of campaign being played. If its a Murder Mystery, a Rogue will be more useful than a Barbarian. If it set in a city, a Bard might have more success than a Ranger.

#### Party Synergy
Inquire what the party you're playing with has, perhaps you will want to compliment them. Generally, I allow duplicate classes if whoever picked it first is okay with it, but never duplicate subclasses or duplicate multiclassing. This is to make sure that each character can feel distinct and special, as well as prevent a Party from being incapable of overcoming certain obstacles.

### Subclasses
Every class gets a "Subclass" at Level 3. These can greatly alter the features of a Class like granting Martial Classes (Weapon Fighters) Spellcasting or amplifying Spellcaster's abilities.

It is usually wise to know what subclass you plan to take even if you aren't Level 3 yet as it is such a crucial part of a character. You can find a Class's Subclasses on the Class page at their Level 3 section.
