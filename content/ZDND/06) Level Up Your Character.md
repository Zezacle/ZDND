When levelling up your character, there's a decent number of things you must remember. Here's a rough checklist to help you not miss anything!

### Level & Keystones
Increase your level and reset your [[Keystones Progression]] to the appropriate amount for the next level.

### Health
#### Hit Points
Check your Class page and increase your Max HP and Current HP by the Level Up Health amount. If you have the [[Tough]] feat and/or Dwarven Toughness, remember to add that bonus as well!

#### Hit Dice
Increase your Current Hit Dice and Max Hit Dice by 1!

### Proficiency Bonus
Check your class table to see if you Proficiency Bonus went up! If so, make sure to update:
- Saving Throw and Skill Modifiers
	- Passive Skill Scores
- Weapon and Spell Attack Bonuses
- Spell Save DC
- Class Features based on Proficiency Bonus
- Feats that allow you add PB to certain things

### Class Features
Check your Class Table to see if you got any new Class Features!

#### Old Class Features
Check to see if any old Class Features improved at this new level!

#### Subclass Features
See if any Subclass Features improved at this new level!

#### General Feat
If you are acquire an Ability Score Increase through a General Feat make sure to increase related skills and attributes.

### Species Traits
See if you have species traits that unlock or improve at your new level!

### Spellcasting
Check your Spellcasting Table to see if you get more prepared spells or spell slots!

#### New Spells from Spell List
If you got unlocked higher Spell Slots, check out your spell list for new spells you can use!

#### Changing Spells
See if your Class changes their spells on Level Up!

#### Species Spells
If you species grants you spells, see if you unlock new ones!

## Inform Your DM!
With new values, your DM might need to update their notes!

Send them:
- New Max HP
- Passive Skill Scores (if they changed)
	- Perception
	- Insight
	- Investigation
- [[Evasion Class]] and [[Armor Class]] (if they changed)
- Any Feats you took
- Any big new features they should be aware of
	- New Languages
