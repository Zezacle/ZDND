Now that you've chosen a [[Classes|Class]] (and Subclass), [[Species]], [[Backgrounds|Background]] and Determined Your Ability Scores, you're ready to fill in your character sheet. As an example, I will be making a Character Sheet for a Level 1 [[Fiendlings|Fiendling]] [[Paladin]] with a [[Knight Errant]] background based on this character art from [RobustaArt](https://www.artstation.com/robusta_art).

![[Lady Lillian Harryn.png|300]] ![[Lady Lillian Harryn - Posed.png|300]]

Throughout this example character build you'll see me jump around and leave certain sections incomplete. This is because multiple sections influence other sections, and its easier to leave certain sections for later. Here's the rough order:

- Name, Background, Species, Class, Subclass, Level
- Ability Scores
	- Leave Skill/Save Modifiers Blank
- Health and Hit Dice
- Proficiency Bonus
	- Saving Throw Proficiencies
	- Skill Proficiencies
	- Armor Training and Weapon Proficiencies
	- Tool Proficiencies
- Species Traits
	- Size and Speed
	- Languages
- Feats
- Equipment
	- Coins
	- Defensive Stats (EC / AC)
	- Weapons
- Class Features
	- Spells and Spellcasting
- Skill Modifiers
	- Initiative
	- Passive Skill Scores
- Personality and Picture

### Ability Scores (Stats)
For this example character, **Lady Lillian Harryn**, I will be using **Point Buy** to determine my Ability Scores. As a [[Paladin]], I need to focus **Strength** or **Dexterity** for my fighting and **Charisma** for my Spellcasting. Additionally, as a [[Fiendlings|Fiendling]], I get to choose where my Racial Bonus goes.

As I want to characterize this Lady Harryn as a nimble fighter who is a bit aloof, I will be focusing **Dexterity** and dumping **Wisdom**.

![[Point Buy Example.png]]

And I'll go ahead and fill in those Ability Scores on my Sheet as well as my Character's Name, Background, Class, Species and planned Subclass. As well as mark our current level, which is 1. The "milestones" section should read "keystones" but I'm too lazy to change it. [[Keystones Progression|Keystones]] are essentially XP Chunks and are how we measure level up progress. At level 1, we need 1 keystone to level up.

![[tutorial1.png]]

### Health
Next, its time to go to my Class page and look at the Core Class Traits table at the top. This table has some important information for character creation. Here we'll pull our Starting [[Hit Points]] and [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]]. Each class has a Hit Point Die value (d6, d8, d10 or d12) and you get one for each level you are. These are used for healing while resting.

![[tutorial2.png]]

### Proficiency
Proficiency is what your character is good at. When you perform skills, saving throws or use weapons you are proficient in, you can add your Proficiency Bonus, which is something that increases as you level up. Per every Class Features table, a Level 1 Character's Proficiency Bonus is +2.

As a [[Paladin]], my Saving Throw Proficiencies are in Wisdom and Charisma. I also get to choose my Skill Proficiencies from my class. Paladins get to pick 2 from 6 choices, so I'll pick [[Athletics]] and [[Persuasion]]. I'll mark these using the little tick bubbles next to the skills.

I'll use the Equipment Training & Proficiencies section under the skills to record my Armor Training, which as a Paladin is everything, and my Weapon Proficiencies which again is both Simple and Martial Weapons.

Now is also a good time to check my chosen Background as that also provides Skill and Tool Proficiencies. As a [[Knight Errant]], I get [[Animal Handling]], [[Persuasion]] and a [[Gaming Sets|Gaming Set]]. 

Since I already had [[Persuasion]] from my class, I'll choose a different skill afforded to me by my class that I don't already have, like [[Insight]].

![[tutorial3.png]]

### Species Traits
Species Traits are extra features granted by your chosen [[Species]]. As I've chosen [[Fiendlings|Fiendling]], I get some spells with other features. I'll write these down in the Species Traits section.

#### Languages
Your Species also determines your known languages. There's a Languages section on page two of the sheet. All player characters are assumed fluent in Common, so you don't need to write that if you don't wish to.

#### Size and Speed
Your species also determines your [[Size]] and base [[Speed]], or how many far you can move on a turn. There's a section near the top for this, but you may get bonuses to your speed from other sources.

### Feats
Feats are special kinds of upgrades to your character. You get a special [[Origin Feats|Origin Feat]] as part of your background and can pick [[General Feats]] at levels 4, 8, 12, and 16.

My background's Origin Feat is [[Skilled]] which lets me choose 3 additional Skill or Tool proficiencies. Yours might grant bonuses to certain things we've already filled in like your [[Hit Points]], so don't forget to update those.

I'm going to choose [[Perception]], [[Investigation]] and [[Tattooist's Tools]] and write this down in my Feats section.

![[tutorial4.png]]

### Equipment
Next I'll pull equipment from our Background and Class. I'll write all the equipment but the weapons in my Equipment section and then add up the provided gold and note it in the Coins section.

![[tutorial5.png]]

#### Defensive Stats
Now that I have Armor, I can calculate our [[Evasion Class]] and [[Armor Class]] and fill out the EC / AC section at the top. Reference the [[Evasion Class]] rules for how to calculate that, and then each Armor has the formula for your AC.

With a [[Brigandine]] (which is Medium Armor) and +2 Dexterity modifier, my [[Evasion Class|EC]] is 12 and my AC is 15. I don't have a [[Shields|Shield]], but if I did, I would mark that and add the bonus it would provide.

#### Weapons
I will add my weapons to the Weapons and Damage Cantrips section and calculate each weapon's Attack Bonus, write down the damage formula and type as well as any notes for those weapons like any [[Weapon Properties]].

For the Attack Bonus, the formula is Proficiency Bonus (if proficient with the weapon) + Strength modifier (if melee) or Dexterity modifier (if ranged, thrown or [[Finesse]]).

The damage formula is the Weapon's listed damage + Strength or Dexterity modifier (same one used for the attack bonus).

![[tutorial6.png]]

Since my Warhammer's properties change based on if I'm striking with one hand or two, I'll list it as two separate weapons.

As I'm making a Dexterity build, I don't actually have the Strength to properly wield a Warhammer, so I can ask the DM if I can switch weapons, or wait until play to sell it and buy a new weapon.

### Class Features
Now on to Class Features. These are often long and you might want to find a shorthand that works for you. Personally, I like:

> Feature Name (Cast Time) - X/X uses per Rest
> Description

![[tutorial7.png]]

Additionally, your Class Features might influence your skills, or other aspects of your character, so edit those as necessary.

#### Spells and Spellcasting
If your class or race provides Spellcasting, you'll need to reference the associated Spellcasting information to determine your Spellcasting Ability (INT, WIS or CHR) and modifier, your Spell Save DC (8 + Proficiency Bonus + Spellcasting Ability Modifier) and your Spell Attack Bonus (Proficiency Bonus + Spellcasting Ability Modifier).

As a Paladin, my Spellcasting Ability is Charisma and my Charisma Modifier is +3. That means my Spell Save DC is 13 and my Spell Attack Modifier is +5.

Your Class table will also tell you how many Spell Slots you have available and which levels they are.

Finally, you can select from your Class Spell List your spells based on how many Prepared/Known spells you have and list these in the Cantrips and Prepared Spells section.

![[tutorial8.png]]

### Saving Throw and Skill Modifiers
Next, I'll fill in Saving Throw and Skill Modifiers. To calculate these, its the same as the associated Ability Score's modifier, then add your Proficiency Bonus if proficient, half your Proficiency Bonus if [[Half Proficiency|Half Proficient]], or double your PB if you have Skill [[Expertise]]. These modifiers can be negative.

For example, with proficiency in [[Athletics]], my Athletics mod is 0 (Strength modifier) + 2 (Proficiency Bonus) for a total of +2.

#### Initiative
Initiative is the bonus you add to the roll you make to decide turn order in combat. It is usually your Dexterity modifier, but may also include your Proficiency Bonus if you have the [[Alert]] feat.

#### Passive Skill Scores
Three skills have "Passive Scores" for the DM to use. These are [[Insight]], [[Investigation]] and most importantly [[Perception]]. To calculate these, its simply 10 + the skill's modifier. There is a section for [[Perception]] under your Health section as its the most used passive score.

![[tutorial9.png]]

### Personality and Picture
This part is optional, as its simply a reminder for you. You can make notes about the character's personality and add a portrait if you have one.

#### CAUTION!
If you are using PDF Gear as your .pdf editor, adding an image will break some background elements and create lots of visual artifacts. Recommend backing up your sheet first or maybe using a different editor to add an image.
