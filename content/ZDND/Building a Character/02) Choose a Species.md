Your "[[Species]]" is what kind of person you are, from Men, Elves, Beastfolk and more. Your "Race" is more akin to a subspecies. The world of ZD&D has a number of species pulled from classic and homebrew D&D all remixed to fit the **Collapse Realms** setting. Your Species and Race will determine much about your character, from their physical build, native language, special traits and Ability Score Bonuses.

### What Species Should I Pick?
Your species is very influential to your character, as it can inform your character's culture, origin and much more. If roleplaying is a key aspect of the game for you, its is recommended you choose a Species on these extrinsic attributes.

If this isn't as interesting, you may find the intrinsic value of certain species, like their Stat Bonuses or Special Features more appealing. In that case, it is recommended to choose a species that grants a Stat Bonus that is useful to your class.

#### Men
Men are the species most similar to real life Humans, including [[Humans]]. They are the most adaptable Species and include Half-Blood races. Men are found in almost all parts of the Collapsed Realms.

| [[Halflings]]  | You choose!   | Think Hobbits! Small and lucky buggers.                 |
| [[Half-Elf]]   | You choose!   | Humans + a kind of Elf                                  |
| [[Half-Giant]] | You choose!   | Big Humans                                              |
| [[Half-Orc]]   | You choose!   | Humans + [[Orcs\|Orc]]                                  |
| [[Fiendlings]] | You choose!   | Human or Halfling tainted by the infernals. (Tieflings) |
| [[Godkin]]     | You choose!   | Human or Halfling blessed by the gods (Aasimaar)        |
#### Elves
Elves are the kind of elves you find in Tolkien's Lord of the Rings or The Elder Scrolls. Tall, lithe, magical. In the Collapsed Realms, they can trace their lineage to Alfheim. In the Collapsed Realms, Elves mostly stay within their own territories claimed by their people, but may invite others into their settlements.

| Races of Elves   | Ability Bonus   |                                                        |
| ---------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| [[Fae]] Elves    | +2 Int, +1 Wis  | High elves. Intelligent magic wielders of great poise. |
| [[Grae]] Elves   | +2 Wis, +1 Str  | Dark elves. Cave dwelling warriors.                    |
| [[Mortae]] Elves | +2 Wis, + 1 Dex | Wood elves. Acrobatic and nature attuned.              |

| [[Orcs]]         | +2 Str, +1 Con | Mean, Green (or gray) and Tusked.                   |
#### Jotuns (Giants)
Jotun is the name for the large bodied inhabitants of Jotunheim also known as Giants. True Giants, as in the *really* big ones are not playable, but Lesser Giants are.
