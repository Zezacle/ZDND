Birdfolk are a wide variety of Humanoid birds with appearances resembling many bird species such as Crows, Hawks, Owls and more.

## Birdfolk Features

**Rarity:** Rare
**Native Language:** [[Beast Speech]]

**Age:** 50 years, Mature at 10
**Size:** Medium
- **Height:** 3.5ft - 6.5ft *(100cm - 200cm)*
- **Weight:** 40lbs - 150 lbs *(20kg - 70kg)*
- **Speed:** 25ft *(5 sq.)*, [[Flying|Fly]] 50ft *(10 sq.)*

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1. 

**Bird's Boon.** If you are not already proficient in either the [[Acrobatics]] and [[Perception]] skills, you have [[Half Proficiency]] in those skills.

**Flight.** You have a [[Flying]] speed of 50 feet. To use this speed, you can’t be wearing [[Medium Armor|Medium]] or [[Heavy Armor]].

**Talons.** Your talons are natural weapons, which you can use to make [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]]. If you hit with them, you deal slashing damage equal to 1d4 + your Strength modifier, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an [[Unarmed Strike]].
