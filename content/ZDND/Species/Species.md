### Species Rarities

The table below explains the rarities of races in relation to common society.

| Rarity    | Description                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Common    | These races are everywhere. It is not unusual for a person to interact with multitudes of them in the course of their life.                                       |
| Uncommon  | These races are scarce, but well known enough that seeing one won't necessarily raise eyebrows.                                                                   |
| Rare      | These races are known to common folk, but a commoner is unlikely to see one in the course of their life unless they were to seek them out.                        |
| Very Rare | These races are treated more-so as myths than real to common folk. A member of a very rare race walking into a town might cause a commotion.                      |
| Unique    | These races are unheard of or otherwise treated as mythical by common folk. They are more likely to be confused as a mutant of another race than their own thing. |


### Species Descriptions

#### Beastfolk

Beastfolk are a large collection of various races native to the wilds of [[Vanaheim]]. It is theorized that they were originally simple animals morphed by the latent [[Vanir]] magic scattered within the wilds. The variety of Beastfolk is wide, including [[Lizardfolk]], [[Catfolk]], [[Birdfolk]], [[Harefolk]], [[Turtlefolk]], [[Rodentians]], and more.

#### Draconic
Draconic describes Dragons or races with lineage to the Great Dreki, or the great powerful dragons of old. They are [[Dragonborn]] and [[Kobolds]].

#### Dvergar
Dvergar are the native species of [[Svartalfheim]]. There are three races of Dvergar: [[Dwarves]], [[Gnomes]] and [[Orcs]].

#### Elves
Elves are the primary sapient species of [[Alfheim]]. They are a long-lived species that can be found uncommonly in every realm. They feature tall, lithe bodies with long pointed ears. There are [[Fae]] or 'High' Elves, [[Grae]] or 'Low' Elves and [[Mortae]] the Wood Elves.

#### Goblinoids
Goblinoids are a group of races native to [[Alfheim]], however somehow they have also colonized large areas of [[Midgard]]'s forests and caves. Despite highly varying appearances, [[Goblins]], [[Hobgoblins]] and [[Bugbears]] all share a heritage.

#### Jotuns
Jotuns, also known simply as Giants, are the species native to [[Jotunheim]]. The first beings were the [[Celestial Giants]] that would birth the [[Aesir]] Gods. In the current era, there are the common lesser Giants such as [[Goliaths]] and [[Firbolgs]], as well the feral Trolls. There are legends that some of the Greater Giants survived Thor's massacre.

#### Men
The Men of [[Midgard]] are a hearty and adaptable race of mortals, crafted by the Aesir in their image. There are [[Humans]] and [[Halflings]]. Additionally, Men are susceptible to the influence of both Gods and Fiends resulting in the birth of [[Godkin]] and [[Fiendlings]], respectively.
