### Traits

***Military Insight.*** Herris has a special eye for the flow of battle, allowing him to see opportunities. Herris has 3 Insight he can spend on various Actions, Bonus Actions and Reactions.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Herris makes any combination of two attacks, a reload or a Battlefield order.
- ***Bayonet.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* `dice: 1d20 +3` (+3) to hit, reach 10 ft.
	- *Hit:* `dice: 1d6` (1d6) piercing damage. (Avg. 3)
- ***Brown Bess. (12 shots)*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* `dice: 1d20 +5` (+5) to hit, range 80/320, single target
	- *Hit:* `dice: 2d12 +2` (2d12 + 2) piercing damage. (Avg. 15)
- ***Reload.*** Herris reloads the Brown Bess.
- **Battlefield Order.** Herris spends an Insight to issue a Battlefield Order to a single creature within 60 feet that can hear him. That creature may make two attacks with its Reaction.

***Battlefield Survey.*** Herris regains all spent Insight to a maximum of 3.

### Reactions

***Evasive Maneuver.*** When an ally within 60 ft. of Herris is hit by an attack, he may use his Reaction and spend an Insight to roll a d8 and increase the creature's EC and AC against that attack by an amount equal to the number he roll.
