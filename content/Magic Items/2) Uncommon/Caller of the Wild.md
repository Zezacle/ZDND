#### [[Caller of the Wild]]
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires attunement)*

**Attunement:** Required
**Charges Left:** Recharges at Dawn
> [!checks|no-title clean]
> -

*A curious rounded cube with a protruding whistle-like mouthpiece. The body of the cube is a matrix of runes that can be rotated to make different combinations.*

As an Action you can manipulate the matrix to input a combination and then blow into the whistle. The whistle doesn't make an audible noise to any creature that is not a beast. The whistle attempts to call the creature respective to the selected combination of the matrix. If the selected creature cannot realistically be found in the surrounding area, the call does nothing. When a creature is called, it is neutral or hostile to the caller. 

The matrix combinations are opaque to the user until they use a combination. They can select a specific combination or roll for a random one, by rolling a d10 and a d4. The number of creatures that appear are whatever are available in the area, if any. The table below lists all possible combinations:

|   5  | Minotaur | Horse | Goat | Cow |
|   6  | Crocodile | Snake | Frilled-Neck Lizard | Pseudodragon |
