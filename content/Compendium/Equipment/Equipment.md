Equipment is the common non-magical items that can purchased or crafted fairly easily.

### Armor
[[Armor]] is worn or wielded equipment that increases your [[Armor Class]]. There is 4 types:
- [[Light Armor]]
- [[Medium Armor]]
- [[Heavy Armor]]
- [[Shields]]

### Weapons
[[Weapons]] are divided into 4 types based on the kind of training needed to be proficient:
- Simple Melee
- Simple Ranged
- Martial Melee
- Martial Ranged

### Tools
[[Tools]] are used for crafting, improving outcomes of ability checks or performing certain actions, such as using [[Thieves' Tools]] to pick a lock.

### Poison
Poisons are special items used evoke certain effects against creatures. Each one has a different method of application.

### Gear Packs
Gear Packs are bundles of common useful items.

### Adventuring Gear
Adventuring Gear is a catch all for any item that doesn't fit into the other equipment categories, such as a [[Torch]] or [[Caltrops]].
