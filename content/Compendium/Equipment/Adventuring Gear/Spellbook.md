*Adventuring Gear (Magic Item)*

A [[Spellbook]] is a catalogue of spells written by a mage to be referenced later as part of spellcasting. They are almost always protected with basic Abjuration magic making them inflammable, waterproof and incapable of being tarnished by substances that aren't applied with a writing utensil like an [[Ink Pen]]. 

> [!NOTE] Wizard Spellbooks
> See [[Wizard]] class page for rules for using a [[Spellbook]] as a [[Wizard]].
