*Adventuring Gear (Magic Item)*

A [[Spellbook]] is a catalogue of spells written by a mage to be referenced later as part of spellcasting. They are almost always protected with basic Abjuration magic making them inflammable, waterproof and incapable of being tarnished by substances that aren't applied with a writing utensil like an [[Ink Pen]]. 

> [!NOTE] Wizard Spellbooks
> See [[Wizard]] class page for rules for using a [[Spellbook]] as a [[Wizard]].

#### Spellbook Pages
To non-wizards, the pages of a spellbook function like [[Spell Scroll|Spell Scrolls]], however when casting spells from spellbook pages, the page must be ripped from the spellbook to be cast. Casting from a spellbook page uses the [[Spell Scroll]] casting rules destroys the spellbook page including any other spells that were written on the page. Unlike [[Spell Scroll|Spell Scrolls]], spellbook pages do not contain Material components, and they must be provided at the time of casting, otherwise the spell fails and the page is consumed.

Page 1:
- [[Magic Missile]]
- [[Web]]

Page 2:
- [[Crystal Crown]]
- [[Fireball]]
