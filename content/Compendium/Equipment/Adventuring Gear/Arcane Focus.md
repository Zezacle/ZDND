*Adventuring Gear*

**Cost:** 15 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb. (crystal), 3 lb. (orb), 2 lb. (rod), 4 lb. ([[Quarterstaff]]), 1 lb. (wand)

An [[Arcane Focus]] is a Crystal, Orb, Rod, [[Quarterstaff]] or Wand that is bejeweled or carved to channel arcane magic. A [[Sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]] can use such an item as a Spellcasting Focus.
