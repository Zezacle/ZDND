**Cost:** 5 cp / 10 pack
**Weight:** 0.5 lb. / 10 pack

[[Sling Bullets]] are used with a [[Sling]] to make a ranged attack. Each time you attack with the weapon, you expend one piece of ammunition.

Drawing the ammunition from a case, pouch, or other container is part of the attack (you need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon).

**Generic Shape.** Many items can double as [[Sling Bullets]], such as rocks of a suitable weight and shape.

**Unrecoverable.** This ammunition is lost when expended.
