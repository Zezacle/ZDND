**Cost:** 1 GP / 10 pack
**Weight:** 0.5 lb. / 10 pack

[[Needles]] are used with a [[Blowpipe]] to make a ranged attack. Each time you attack with the weapon, you expend one piece of ammunition.

Drawing the ammunition from a case, pouch, or other container is part of the attack (you need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon).

**Unrecoverable.** This ammunition is lost when expended.
