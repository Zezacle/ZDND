**Cost:** 5 GP / 10 pack
**Weight:** 1 lb. / 10 pack

[[Firearm Bullets]] are used with a Firearm to make a ranged attack. Each time you attack with the weapon, you expend one piece of ammunition.

This ammunition item includes the projectile and the propellant used in firing the weapon.

**Unrecoverable.** This ammunition is lost when expended.
