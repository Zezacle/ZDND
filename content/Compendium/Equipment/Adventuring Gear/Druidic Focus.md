*Adventuring Gear*

**Cost:** 5 GP
**Weight:** 4 lb. ([[Quarterstaff]]), 1 lb. (wand), 0.5 lb. (mistletoe)

A [[Druidic Focus]] is a [[Quarterstaff]], Wand or sprig of mistletoe carved, tied with ribbon, or painted to channel primal magic. A [[Druid]] or [[Ranger]] can use such an object as a Spellcasting Focus.
