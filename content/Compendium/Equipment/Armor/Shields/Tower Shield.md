#### [[Tower Shield]] (10 GP)
*Shield (Requires Shield Training)*

**Weight:** 15 lb.
**Bonuses:** 
- +2 [[Armor Class|AC]]
- +2 [[Evasion Class|EC]]
**Properties:**
- **Cover.** When you take the [[Dodge]] action while wielding a [[Tower Shield]], it provides [[Three-Quarters Cover]].
- **Sluggish.** Each time you [[Attack]] while carrying a [[Tower Shield]] your speed is reduced by 5 feet.
- [[Unstealthy]]
