**Cost:** 35 GP
**Weight:** 2 lb.

**Armor Class (AC):** +1 AC

You cannot use a shield while wearing this gauntlet.

When wearing the [[Swordcatcher's Gauntlet]], if you are partially hit (above [[Evasion Class|EC]], below [[Armor Class|AC]]) by a melee weapon attack, you can use your Reaction to make a [[Disarm]] check against the creature's weapon.
