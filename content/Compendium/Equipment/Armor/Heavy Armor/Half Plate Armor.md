#### [[Half Plate Armor]] (800 GP)
*Heavy Armor (10 Minutes to Don and 5 Minutes to Doff)*

**Armor Class (AC):** 18
**Weight:** 45 lb.
**Strength Requirement:** 13
**Properties:**
- *Special:*
	- **Halfway There.** Providing this armor to an Armor Smith reduces the cost of crafting [[Three-Quarters Plate Armor]] or [[Full Plate Armor]] Armor by 700 GP.
	- **Divisible.** With [[Leatherworker's Tools]] and 1 hour of work (or half as much if you are proficient with [[Leatherworker's Tools]]), this armor can be converted to 1 [[Cuirass]] and 1 [[Plate Harness]].
- [[Hefty]]
- [[Unstealthy]]
