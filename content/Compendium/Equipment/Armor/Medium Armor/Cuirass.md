#### [[Cuirass]] (400 GP)
*Medium Armor (5 Minutes to Don and 1 Minute to Doff)*

**Armor Class (AC):** 15 + Dexterity modifier (Max 2)
**Weight:** 25 lb.
**Strength Requirement:** 9
**Properties:**
- *Special:* 
	- **Partway There.** Providing this armor to an Armor Smith reduces the cost of crafting [[Half Plate Armor]], [[Three-Quarters Plate Armor]], or [[Full Plate Armor]] Armor by 350 GP.
	- **Part of a Whole.** If you have [[Leatherworker's Tools]] and are proficient with them, you can spend 1 hour to combine a [[Cuirass]] and a [[Plate Harness]] into [[Half Plate Armor]] Armor.
- [[Cumbersome]]
- [[Unstealthy]]
