*Inhaled Poison*

**Cost:** 250 GP

A creature subjected to [[Burnt Othur Fumes]] must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 3d6 Poison damage, and it must repeat the save at the start of each of its turns. On each successive failed save, the creature takes 1d6 Poison damage. After three successful saves, the poison ends.
