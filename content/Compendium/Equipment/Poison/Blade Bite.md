*Injury Poison*

**Cost:** 25 GP

A creature subjected to [[Blade Bite]] makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 1d8 Poison damage and has the [[Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

[[Blade Bite]] evaporates into a harmless residue after 1 minute of application unless enclosed, such as in an ammunition case or sword sheath.
