*Contact Poison*

**Cost:** 10 GP

A Fiend or Undead subjected to [[Holy Water]] makes a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 2d8 Radiant damage. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

When you take the [[Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of [[Holy Water]]. Additionally, you can use the [[Quick Toss]] bonus action.
