When a player character would otherwise be killed, the DM may elect to allow them to have a [[Permanent Injury]] instead.

As an alternative to dying, apply one of the following permanent Injuries, either by rolling a d10 or choosing an method appropriate for the injury or seems like the appropriate hindrance for the character:

| d10 |   Permanent Injury   |                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :-: | :------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  |     **Limping**      | *Your leg isn't what it used to be.* Your [[Speed]] is reduced by 5 feet.                                                                                                                          |
|  2  |     **One-Eyed**     | *You're missing an eye.* You have [[Disadvantage]] on ranged attacks closer than 30 feet.                                                                                                          |
|  3  |      **Trauma**      | *You startle easily.* You have [[Disadvantage]] on [[Initiative]] rolls.                                                                                                                           |
|  4  |   **Cracked Ribs**   | *Your ribs hurt when you run.* When you take the [[Dash]] action, you take 2d6 damage.                                                                                                             |
|  5  | **Irregular Heart**  | *Your heart can skip a beat.* You have [[Disadvantage]] on Constitution saving throws.                                                                                                             |
|  6  |   **Bad Shoulder**   | *Your should hinders quick movement.* You have [[Disadvantage]] on Dexterity saving throws.                                                                                                        |
|  7  | **Fractured Skull**  | *Your mind fails you at the worst of times.* You have [[Disadvantage]] on Wisdom and Intelligence saving throws.                                                                                   |
|  8  |     **Tremors**      | *Your hands won't stop shaking.* You have Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks that use dexterity.                                                                                      |
|  9  |   **Phantom Pain**   | *Your wound keeps acting up.* When you take damage, you take additional damage equal to your proficiency bonus.                                                                                    |
| 10  | **Inevitable Fault** | *You're a ticking time bomb.* When you take more than 25% of your maximum [[Hit Points]] worth of damage in one blow, roll a d10. On a 1, you reduce to 0 Hit Points and start making Death Saves. |
A Permanent Injury can be healed with the [[Greater Restoration]] or [[Regenerate]] spells.
