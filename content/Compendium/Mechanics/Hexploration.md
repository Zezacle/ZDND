[[Private/Mechanics/Hexploration]] is the process of over-land travel used when the travel may have unexpected discoveries, encounters or other interesting things. It consists of moving across a hex grid, discovering what that hex contains, previewing the neighboring hexes and making an informed decision on where to go next.

Hexploration rules are not used for traveling long distances between known points over known terrain. (We simplify that shiiii cuz)

### 3-Mile Hexes

When using Hexploration rules, each hex is one [[League]] or 3-miles in diameter.

Travelling a [[League]] on foot varies in time based on the terrain-type of the hex you are entering:
- **Roads.** 1 hour. (see below)
- **Plains or Hills.** 2 hours.
- **Bog or Forest.** 4 hours.
- **Mountains.** 8 hours.

#### Roads
Some hexes may be connected by roads. If you are travelling on a road, it takes 1 hour to enter a hex with a connected road. If you are entering a hex that has a road, but are not yet on the road, you must use the appropriate terrain travel time.

#### Mountain Passages
Certain Mountain Ranges have more accessible crossings similar to roads. Travelling through Mountain hexes along a mountain passage takes 4 hours instead of 8.

#### Crossing Water
Rivers along hex-lines are shallow and narrow enough to cross on foot and do not interfere with travel. Rivers or other kinds of water that appear in the center of hexes require an explicit way to cross such as a boat or bridge.

#### Seeing the Land
While in one hex, you can see into neighboring hexes, provided your vision is not obscured by terrain, darkness, etc. Elevation may allow you to see into further hexes, but Dense Forests or Mountains explicitly obscure hexes beyond them.

#### Searching the Land
While in a hex, you can search it for anything hidden inside, such as a cave, obscured structure, abandoned campsite, etc. Searching a hex takes as long as entering it (see [[League]] travel times above).

### Cartography (Optional Rule)
Certain campaigns might use the Cartography rule, which state that unless someone in the party is mapping the hexes they traverse, the party "forgets" the land they've traveled after a sleeping for the night and the hexes are hidden again on the map. With these rules, the party can only fill in the obscured hex map if someone in their group is regularly using [[Cartographer's Tools]].

#### Chart the Land
At the end of a day of travel, one person in the party can spend 1 hour using [[Cartographer's Tools]] to chart the land they've traversed and permanently add it to the party's map. If this is not done, the hexes are obscured again at the end of their Rest.

Whoever does this makes a History check (DC 15) and can add their proficiency bonus if they are proficient with [[Cartographer's Tools]]. On a success, every hex revealed during that day of travel is added to the map. On a fail, only the hexes the party entered is added to the map.

## Adventuring Time Management
An adventuring day usually consists of 12 hours of adventuring, which could be travel, dungeon crawling, etc., 4 hours of downtime which can be spent on [[Downtime Tasks]], and 8 hours for Resting.

If the party elects to continue adventuring past 12 hours, every 2 hours that they travel, they must succeed a Constitution saving throw or be subjected to to [[Exhaustion]]. If there is additional challenging circumstances, such as inclement weather, or the party is injured, they may be required to make their saves every hour.

### Foraging
If the party has downtime, a party member may elect to spend the downtime hunting or foraging for food, by making a [[Survival]] check. If they are successful, the party does not expend a day's ration.

#### Foraging DC by Terrain
The DC for the Survival check is dependent on the terrain of the hex the party is in.

| DC  | Terrain Type              |
| --- | ------------------------- |
| 12  | Forest                    |
| 14  | Wetlands, Plains or Hills |
| 16  | Mountains                 |
| 18  | Desert                    |

#### Foraging DC Modifiers
The DC for the Survival check can be altered based on the factors listed below. Multiple can apply at once. (Negative numbers are good for players.)

| DC Mod. | Modifying Element                                 |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| -4      | Water source (River, Lake, Sea)                   |
| -2      | [[Cook's Utensils]] proficiency (anyone in party) |
| +2      | Inclement Weather                                 |
| +4      | Land Affliction (Pollution/Curse)                 |
