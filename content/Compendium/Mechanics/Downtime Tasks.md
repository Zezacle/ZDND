A [[Downtime Tasks|Downtime Task]] is a project, job or study that a character must devote substantial, tedious time to complete. Such a task has an associated **Task Difficulty (DC)**, **Number of Hours** to complete, and often a task has **Prerequisites** such as location, mentorship, or material cost.'

Completion of the task grants it's **Reward.**

### Time Cost

Working on a Downtime Task is measured in blocks of 4 hours. If a character works more than 8 hours without resting, they gain a point of [[Exhaustion]] at the end of the work block.

### Task Difficulty

Each time a character works on their task for 4 hours, they can roll an associated Ability Check, on a success they reach a 'breakthrough', and the time they worked counts as double towards the total time needed. They have [[Advantage]] on this Ability Check if they have a mentor or guidebook helping them.

### Prerequisites

A character cannot begin to progress on a task unless they have met all the prerequisites. Some tasks can only be progressed in certain locations, such as a workshop. If a task has a Material Cost prerequisite, the first 4 hours can be progressed without it.

### Example Tasks

#### Learning Lockpicking
**Reward:** Thieves' Tools Proficiency
- **Time:** 60 hours
- **Difficulty:** 16
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- [[Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]]
	- Lock (Material, 10gp)

#### Repairing a Magic Weapon
**Reward:** Magic Weapon
- **Time:** 60 hours
- **Difficulty:** 18
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- Workshop and [[Smith's Tools|Smith's Tools]]
	- [[Smith's Tools|Smith's Tools]] Proficiency
	- Varying Material Cost

#### Writing a Regional History Book
**Reward:** A book titled "History of (Region)" worth 50gp, [[Advantage]] on Intelligence (History) checks related to the region
- **Time:** 100 hours
- **Difficulty:** 14
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- [[Calligrapher's Supplies|Calligrapher's Supplies]]
	- Source Material (be in the region or have other knowledge sources)
	- Blank Book (Material, 5gp)

#### Working the Lumberyard
**Reward:** 10gp
- **Time:** 8 hours
- **Difficulty:** 12
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- Lumberyard
	- Strength 12 or higher

#### Practice Spellcasting
**Reward:** 1 Level-up Milestone
- **Time:** 40 hours
- **Difficulty:** 16
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- Spellcasting Feature
	- 2x Spell Slots per 4 hours
	- Can't be done more than once / character level

#### Train with Weapons
**Reward:** 1 Level-up Milestone
- **Time:** 24 hours
- Difficulty: 12
- **Prerequisites:** 
	- Can't work more than 4 hours / day
	- Can't be done more than once / character level
