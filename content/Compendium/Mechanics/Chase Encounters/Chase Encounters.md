A Chase Encounter is when a PC is involved with running after or away from an NPC, usually by using the [[Sprint]] action. Chases are measured by distance and are a magnet for Chase Hazards.

To perform a chase you need to:
- Determine the Environment and how that affects the chase.
	- Action, Cover and Distance features
	- Environmental Features
	- Environmental Hazard Flavor
- Establish a 'Zero Point' and track the distance of the chase.
- Roll Chase Hazards each time a character attempts the Sprint action.
- Roll for Exhaustion at the end of each Sprint.

The chase ends when the PCs either lose the target or catch up to them and use their action to kill or otherwise subdue them.

### Chase Hazards
When a creature tries to take the [[Sprint]] action during a chase, the DM rolls on the [[Chase Hazards]] table and describes the hazard in such a way that it fits with the environment of the chase, adjudicating the results before the creature moves the distance of its [[Sprint]].

***Determining DCs.*** When making a saving throw or ability check as a part of a Chase Hazard, the DC is equal to 8 + the opponent's proficiency bonus + the opponent's highest ability score modifier.

***Determining Damage.*** When a Chase Hazard deals damage, the DM selects an appropriate type. The amount of damage a hazard deals is equal to 1d6 times the opponent's proficiency bonus.

***Escalating Hazards (Optional Rule).*** Add +1 to your result on the Hazard Table for every round the chase has been going on for.

### Tracking Distance
The DM and players track movement together in a chase, beginning by establishing a 'zero point.' All movement in a chase is tracked from that point. Each player is responsible for tracking the location and movement of their player character, with the DM being responsible for the NPCs.

**Chases from Scratch.** If the chase encounter began in roleplay or exploration, your zero point is the original location of the quarry before they took the [[Sprint]] action.

**Chases from Combat.** If the chase encounter began from a combat, your zero point is the edge of the battlemap that the quarry ran off from.
