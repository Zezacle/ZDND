Chases are defined by their surroundings. Create unique chases by customizing the environments they take place in.

### GENERAL FEATURES
Choose one feature from each of the following sections to define the specifics of the environment.

#### ACTION FEATURES
Action features represent an environment making certain actions easier to perform than normal: 
- **Clear.** You can [[Search]] as a Bonus Action. 
- **Shrouded.** You can [[Hide]] as a Bonus Action. 
- **Smooth.** You can [[Dash]] as a Bonus Action.

#### COVER FEATURES
Clutter can make ranged weapons difficult to use: 
- **Open.** There are no opportunities for cover. 
- **Normal.** Creatures beyond 30 feet gain the benefits of [[Half Cover]]. Creatures beyond 100 feet gain the benefits of [[Three-Quarters Cover]]. 
- **Cluttered.** Creatures have [[Three-Quarters Cover]] against all ranged attacks.

#### DISTANCE FEATURES
Certain environments make it easier or harder to keep an eye on the quarry:
- **Sparse.** Opponents are unseen beyond 500 ft. 
- **Normal.** Opponents are unseen beyond 300 ft. 
- **Dense.** Opponents are unseen beyond 100 ft.

### Environment Features
Choose one or more of the following features that best describe the chase's environment.

#### Arctic
Creatures that are not [[Resistance|Resistant]] or [[Immunity|Immune]] to Cold damage have [[Disadvantage]] on the saving throw to avoid [[Exhaustion]] while [[Sprint|Sprinting]]. 

#### Coastal
Creatures have [[Advantage]] on the saving throw to avoid [[Exhaustion]] while [[Sprint|Sprinting]]. 

#### Desert
Creatures that are not [[Resistance|Resistant]] or [[Immunity|Immune]] to Fire damage have [[Disadvantage]] on the saving throw to avoid [[Exhaustion]] while [[Sprint|Sprinting]]. 

#### Forest
Dexterity ([[Stealth]]) checks made as part of the [[Hide]] action have [[Advantage]].

#### Grassland
Creatures have [[Advantage]] on the saving throw to avoid [[Exhaustion]] while [[Sprint|Sprinting]]. 

#### Hills 
Creatures that lack a climb [[Speed]] make two [[Exhaustion]] saving throws every time they take the [[Sprint]] action.

#### Mountains
When a creature takes damage from a hazard in the mountains, it falls 20 feet and takes 2d6 additional damage.

#### Swam
Creatures that lack a swim [[Speed]] make two [[Exhaustion]] saving throws every time they take the [[Sprint]] action.

#### Underdark
Creatures without [[Darkvision]] add +2 to rolls on the Hazard table.

#### Underwater
Creatures that lack a swim [[Speed]] move at half [[Speed]].

#### Urban
When a creature rolls a natural 1 on an attack roll, the attack hits a random creature in the urban environment.
