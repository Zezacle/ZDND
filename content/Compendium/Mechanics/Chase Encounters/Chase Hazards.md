### Hazards

#### Bad Gases
**D20 Test:** Constitution saving throw
**Failure:** You take damage and are wheezing. Repeat this save at the end of your next turn. **Success:** You take half damage and must no longer repeat the saving throw.
**Success or Failure:** You have the [[Poisoned]] condition until you take 10 minutes to catch your breath.

#### Climbable
**D20 Test:** Strength ([[Athletics]])
****Failure:**** You fall 20 feet and land [[Prone]]. Standing up costs half your [[Speed]] as normal.
**Success:** You move an additional 20 feet this turn.

#### Confusing Area
**D20 Test:** Wisdom ([[Survival]])
**Failure:** You lose sight of the opponent.
**Success:** You move an additional 20 feet this turn.

#### Crossing Paths
**D20 Test:** Charisma ([[Intimidation]] or [[Persuasion]])
**Failure:** The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is halved.
**Success:** You move an additional 20 feet this turn.

#### Crowd Swarms
**D20 Test:** Wisdom ([[Insight]])
**Failure:** You lose sight of the opponent. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is halved.
**Success:** You can [[Dash]] as a Bonus Action. If you do, you also gain the benefits of the [[Dodge]] action.

#### Dead End
**D20 Test:** Intelligence ([[Investigation]])
**Failure:** You lose sight of the opponent. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced to 0.
**Success:** You can [[Dash]] as a Bonus Action.

#### Difficult Terrain
**D20 Test:** Wisdom ([[Perception]])
**Failure:** The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action is halved until you succeed on a [[D20 Test]] relating to a hazard.
**Success:** You automatically succeed on the saving throw to avoid [[Exhaustion]] as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn.

#### Ground Collapse
**D20 Test:** Dexterity saving throw
**Failure:** You take damage and fall into a 20-foot hole, landing [[Prone]]. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced to 0. 
**Success:** You can [[Dash]] as a bonus action.

#### Hazardous Gap
**D20 Test:** Strength ([[Athletics]])
**Failure:** You take damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be [[Stunned]] until the end of your next round.
**Success:** You can [[Dash]] as a bonus action.

#### Medium Damage
**D20 Test:** Dexterity saving throw
**Failure:** You take damage. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is halved. 
**Success:** Choose an opponent. They take half the amount of damage you would have taken.

#### Minor Obfuscation
**D20 Test:** Wisdom ([[Perception]])
**Failure:** You lose sight of all opponents. 
**Success:** All opponents lose sight of you.

#### Minor Obstruction
**D20 Test:** Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]])
**Failure:** The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced by 30 feet. 
**Success:** You move an additional 30 feet this turn.

#### Moving Hazard
**D20 Test:** Wisdom saving throw.
**Failure:** You take damage and fall [[Prone]]. Standing up costs half your [[Speed]] as normal.
**Success:** You take half as much damage only.

#### Painful Danger
**D20 Test:** Strength saving throw
**Failure:** You take damage, and are vulnerable to it. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced to 0.
**Success:** You take half as much damage only.

#### Sudden Spillage
**D20 Test:** Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]])
**Failure:** You lose sight of all opponents and fall [[Prone]].
**Success:** You move an additional 20 feet this turn.

#### The Maze
**D20 Test:** Intelligence saving throw. 
**Failure:** The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced to 0. Repeat this hazard on every subsequent turn until you succeed.
**Success:** You move an additional 90 feet this turn.

#### Tiny Damage
**D20 Test:** Any saving throw
**Failure:** You take 1d6 damage.

#### Trip Hazard
**D20 Test:** Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]])
**Failure:** You fall [[Prone]]. The distance you move as part of the [[Sprint]] action this turn is reduced to 0. 
**Success:** You move an additional 20 feet this turn.
