A negotiation is when the party has a plausible request to make of an NPC who is willing to negotiate. The NPC's attitude towards the party will determine their willingness to negotiate, influencing their starting **Interest** in coming to a deal and their **Patience** to keep negotiating.

> [!info] Not Mind Control
> _Negotiation is not a process that changes an NPC's character. Rather, the players are trying to make an NPC understand how behaving differently would be **in character.**_

### Attitudes, Interest and Patience

#### NPC Attitudes
| Attitude   | Interest | Patience |
| ---------- | :------: | :------: |
| Hostile    |    1     |    2     |
| Suspicious |    2     |    2     |
| Neutral    |    2     |    3     |
| Open       |    3     |    3     |
| Friendly   |    3     |    4     |
| Trusting   |    3     |    5     |

#### Interest (0-5)
An NPC's interest represents how eager they are to make a deal with the negotiating party. Interest is graded on a scale of 0 (no interest) to 5 (the most possible interest). When a negotiation begins, an NPC's interest is between 1 and 4. If the NPC's interest reaches 5, they make a final offer and the negotiation ends. If the NPC's interest drops to 0, they end a negotiation without making an offer. Interest increases and decreases during the negotiation.

#### Patience (0-5)
An NPC's patience represents how much time and effort they are willing to devote to a negotiation. Patience is graded on a scale of 0 to 5, with each NPC starting a negotiation with their patience higher than 0. If an NPC's patience reaches 0, the NPC makes a final offer and negotiation ends. Patience can decrease each time the negotiating party makes an argument.

When players make arguments to an NPC as part of a negotiation, they can appeal to various values the NPC might hold to attempt to convince them to grant their request.

#### Motivations (Positive)
Motivations represent values an NPC holds that could be appealed to in a negotiation. Each motivation can only be successfully appealed to once.

#### Pitfalls (Negative)
Pitfalls represent values that an NPC's do NOT identify with, and would be insulted to have these values attributed to them. Arguments mentioning pitfall automatically fail. 

## Negotiating

When players are negotiating, they have three options: Get a read on the NPC ([[Insight]]), Make an argument ([[Persuasion]], [[Intimidation]], [[Deception]]) or Accept an offer (End Negotiation).

When a player makes an ability check as part of a negotiation, the DC is measured in three parts: 11 or lower is a failure, 12 to 16 is a partial failure, 17 or higher is a success.

**When reading an NPC,** Failures reduce their patience by 1, Partial Failures might reveal a Motivation or Pitfall or do nothing, and Successes reveal their Motivations and Pitfalls.

### Making an Argument

When making an argument, the players can attempt to appeal to an NPC's value (Motivation, Pitfall or neither) or tell a lie.

#### Appealing to a Motivation
Make a [[Persuasion]] or [[Intimidation]] check.
- **Failure:** The NPC's patience decreases by 1.
- **DC12 Partial Failure:** The NPC's interest increases by 1 and their patience decreases by 1.
- **DC17 Success:** The NPC's interest increases by 1.

#### Appealing to a Pitfall
The argument automatically fails and the NPC's interest and patience decreases by 1.

#### Appealing to neither Motivation or Pitfall
Make a [[Persuasion]] or [[Intimidation]] check.
- **Failure:** The NPC's interest and patience decreases by 1.
- **DC12 Partial Failure:** The NPC's patience decreases by 1.
- **DC17 Success:** The NPC's interest increases by 1 and their patience decreases by 1.

#### Telling a Lie
In any instance where you would normally make a [[Persuasion]] or [[Intimidation]] check, make a [[Deception]] check instead.

If your lie fails to increase the NPC's interest, you get caught in the lie and the NPC's interest decreases by 1, in addition to any decrease imposed by the failure.

#### Accepting an Offer
You end the negotiation by asking the NPC to make an offer, determined by their current Interest level. This is considered the NPC's final offer, but the players aren't always obliged to accept it.
