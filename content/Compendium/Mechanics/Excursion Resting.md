Standard D&D 5e Resting rules are balanced around 6-8 encounters per day, which doesn't make sense in most stories that aren't constant dungeon crawls or brawls through the frontline of a battlefield. 

[[Excursion Resting]] is a slowed down system of resting to heighten the resource drain inflicted upon players in stories where there is only 1-3 encounters per day. In short, a [[Short Rest]] or "Night's Rest" takes a whole night, and a [[Long Rest]] has specific criteria that needs to be met in order to be completed such as resting in a town or in a prolonged campsite.

## Excursion Resting Rules

### Short Rest or "Night's Rest"

A [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]] can be taken anywhere over the course of 8 hours, 6 of which must be spent sleeping, 2 of which can be light activity, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch. During sleep, you have the [[Unconscious]] condition. After you finish a [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]], you must wait at least 16 hours before starting another one. To start a [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]], you must have at least 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]]. 

***Interrupting the Rest.*** A rest is stopped by the following interruptions:
- Rolling [[Initiative]]
- Casting a spell with a spell slot other than a cantrip
- Taking any damage
- 1 hour of walking or other physical exertion

You can resume a [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]] immediately after an interruption. If you do so, the rest requires 1 additional hour per interruption to finish.

### Long Rest

A [[Long Rest]] can be achieved after taking three [[Short Rest|Night's Rests]] in a row in the same location without interrupting the rest, or in a single [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]] in a **Safe Haven.**

You can resume a [[Long Rest]] immediately after an interruption, however if that interruption was rolling [[Initiative]] and happened outside of a **Safe Haven**, it becomes a [[Short Rest|Night's Rest]] and any streak is lost. 

If you do resume a [[Long Rest]], the rest requires 1 additional hour per interruption to finish.

#### Safe Haven
A **Safe Haven** is a location where the party is safe from all but the most extreme dangers. Example Safe Havens include:
- In a town or allied fort
- In a location that has been secured by the party for at least five consecutive days
- Locations protected by the [[Private Sanctum]] or [[Hallow]] spell
- Inside the [[Magnificent Mansion]]

> [!warning] Safe Havens created by Spells
> If you use a spell slot to cast a spell that creates a Safe Haven, you do not regain that spell slot after your first time Long Resting inside of it.

### Full Rest

A [[Full Rest]] can be achieved after taking three consecutive [[Long Rest|Long Rests]] in the same location (or city). Upon completing a [[Full Rest]], a character gains [[Heroic Inspiration]] and may roll a number of free [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Dice]] equal to half their proficiency bonus (rounded down) and gain an amount of [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the total.

If a character takes enough damage to lose their gained [[Temporary Hit Points|Temp HP]] or uses their [[Heroic Inspiration]], their [[Long Rest]] streak is broken and they cannot regain the benefits of a [[Full Rest]] even if they [[Long Rest]] again at the end of the day.

### Magic Item Recharge

In Excursion Resting rules, magic items that recharge at "[[Holy Dawn]]" aka the dawn of the last day of the week. (eg. Sunday or the setting's equivalent)
