This tattoo is indistinguishable from a scar. By using a [[Tattooist's Tools]], the scar can be moved to another part of the body and its appearance changed. The scar can magically hide hair or skin blemishes if it covers the affected area however any other tattoos, magical or mundane, will appear as if they were tattooed over the scar.

The time it takes to change the tattoo depends on the size of the new scar. Small scars take 10 minutes, Medium scars take 1 hour and Large or full body scars take 4 hours.
