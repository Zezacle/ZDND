At the application of tattoo, a willing creature can expend spell slots with a combined level no higher than 5. Each expended spell slot is stored within the tattoo separately and cannot be combined or separated.

As a Bonus Action, the bearer may transfer a stored Spell Slot to themself or a creature they can see within 30 feet. Once all stored Spell Slots are expended, the tattoo fades from the bearer's skin and is lost.
