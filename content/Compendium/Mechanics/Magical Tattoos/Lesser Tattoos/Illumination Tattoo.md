As a [[Magic]] Action, the bearer can activate the tattoo causing the ink to glow. The tattoo provides 5 feet of [[Bright Light]] and an additional 5 feet of [[Dim Light]]. They can dismiss the effect as a free action.

Additionally, the bearer may expend a charge to bolster the effect, causing the tattoo to provide 15 feet of [[Bright Light]] and an additional 15 feet of [[Dim Light]] for 1 hour. The tattoo has 3 charges and regains all expended charges when you complete a [[Short Rest]].
