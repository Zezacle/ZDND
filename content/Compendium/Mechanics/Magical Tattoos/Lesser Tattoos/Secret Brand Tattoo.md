A [[Secret Brand Tattoo]] is formed into a distinct shape or pattern when applied. Upon completion, the tattoo becomes invisible to anyone who doesn't have a [[Secret Brand Tattoo]] of that exact shape or pattern.

The tattoo can be seen with a [[Detect Magic]] spell, however the shape or pattern is still obscured.

Only [[Truesight]] can reveal the tattoo to a creature that does not share the tattoo.
