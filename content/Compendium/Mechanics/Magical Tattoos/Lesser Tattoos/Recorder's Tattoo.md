As a [[Magic]] Action the bearer may activate the tattoo. The next 30 seconds of sound that the bearer hears is encoded into the tattoo. As a [[Magic]] Action, the bearer may trigger the playback of recorded audio, audible to any creature within 15 feet.

Activating the tattoo again overwrites previously recorded audio.

If the bearer is [[Deafened]] while the tattoo is recording, the recorded audio reflects their auditory perception at the time of recording, which might be nothing, ringing in the ears or other effect.
