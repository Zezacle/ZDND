**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: One Arm
- Level 2: One Arm + Any Torso or other Arm

#### Swiftspell (I)
**Swift Casting.** When you cast a spell that has a casting time of an Action, change the casting time to a Bonus Action for this casting. You can't modify a spell in this way if you've already cast a level 1+ spell on the current turn, nor can you cast a level 1+ spell on this turn after modifying a spell in this way.

Upon using the feature, you take a number of d4s of Force damage equal to the spell slot used. (eg. 2nd level spell slot = 2d4 Force damage.)

**Sorcerer's Swiftspell.** If you use the Quickened Spell metamagic in addition to Swiftspell, you can cast two levelled spells in one turn as long as their combined spell slot levels do not exceed 3. For example you could cast a 2nd level spell and a 1st level, or two 2nd level spells.

#### Swiftspell (II)
**Stabilized Swiftspell.** You no longer take damage when you use the Swift Casting feature.

**Improved Sorcerer's Swiftspell.** If you use the Quickened Spell metamagic in addition to Swiftspell, you can cast two levelled spells in one turn as long as their combined spell slot levels do not exceed 5. For example you could cast a 4th level spell and a 1st level spell, or a 3rd level spell and a 1st level spell.
