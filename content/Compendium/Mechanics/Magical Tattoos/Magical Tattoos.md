*Turn your skin into a bearer of powerful arcana.*

Magical Tattoos are made of magical ink, with intricate patterns and runes woven into the design that can elicit arcane effects. The appearance of the tattoo can be anything, even invisible, however it will always be visible when observed with [[Truesight]], [[Detect Magic]] or similar effects.

Magical Tattoos are broken into two categories, **Greater** and **Lesser** Tattoos, each requiring [[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]] for application.

#### Application of Magical Tattoos
Applying a Tattoo to a body part, magical or not, requires 4 hours of work by someone proficient with a [[Tattooist's Tools]].  You can only self-apply tattoos to your chest, arms and legs. 

[[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]] applied as part of a Magical Tattoo adheres to scales, bone and fur like a permanent dye. Once a Greater Magical Tattoo is complete, the bearer automatically attunes to it. Incomplete Greater Tattoos occupy the body part but do not consume an [[Attunement]] slot until complete. 

#### Removing Magical Tattoos
A [[Greater Restoration]] spell can remove any number or all of Lesser Magical Tattoos or one whole Greater Magical Tattoo from a creature, which breaks the [[Attunement]].

## Greater Magical Tattoos
A Greater Magical Tattoo provides a strong effect and has **Body Part Requirements** for its effects to apply and uses an [[Attunement]] slot. More powerful tattoos may require multiple and/or specific body parts, requiring a vial of [[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]]. 

### Body Part Requirements
Body Parts are broken into groups based on the region of the body with eight parts in total. A tattoo might require the whole region or just a part of it. A body part that bears a Greater Magical Tattoo cannot be used to fulfill a different magic tattoo's requirements.

When upgrading a tattoo's level, the fulfilled Body Part Requirements of the previous level contribute to the fulfillment of the higher level's requirements.

#### Body Parts

| Head  | Torso | Arms      | Legs     |
| ----- | ----- | --------- | -------- |
| Scalp | Chest | Left Arm  | Left Leg |
| Neck  | Back  | Right Arm | Left Arm |

## Lesser Magical Tattoos
Lesser Magical Tattoos are simpler in effect and only ever require one vial of [[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]]. These tattoos do not have any other requirements, with the only restriction being you can only have a number of Lesser Magical Tattoos equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1).
