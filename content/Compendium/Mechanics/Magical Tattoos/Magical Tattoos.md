*Turn your skin into a bearer of powerful arcana.*

Magical Tattoos are made of magical ink, with intricate patterns and runes woven into the design that can elicit arcane effects. The appearance of the tattoo can be anything, even invisible, however it will always be visible when observed with [[Truesight]], [[Detect Magic]] or similar effects.

A Magical Tattoo requires [[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]], one vial per body part the tattoo is applied to, and space on the body as Magical Tattoos cannot overlap. Specific Magical Tattoos require being on certain parts of the body for their effects to apply, with some tattoo's effects being amplified based on the number of body parts it is on. 

#### Application of Magical Tattoos
Applying a Tattoo to a body part requires 4 hours of work by someone proficient with a [[Tattooing Kit]]. [[Enchanted Tattoo Ink]] applied as part of a Magical Tattoo adheres to scales, bone and fur like a permanent dye. You can only self-apply tattoos to your arms and legs. Once a Magical Tattoo is complete, the bearer automatically attunes to it. Incomplete tattoos occupy the body part but do not consume an [[Attunement]] slot. Magical Tattoos and their accompanying [[Attunement]] can only be removed with a [[Greater Restoration]] spell.

### Body Part Requirements
Body Parts are broken into groups based on the region of the body with eight parts in total. A tattoo might require the whole region or just a part of it. A body part that bears a Magical Tattoo cannot be used to fulfill a different magic tattoo's requirements.

When upgrading a tattoo's level, the Body Part Requirements of the previous level contribute to the requirements of the higher level.

#### Body Parts

| Head  | Torso | Arms      | Legs     |
| ----- | ----- | --------- | -------- |
| Scalp | Chest | Left Arm  | Left Leg |
| Neck  | Back  | Right Arm | Left Arm |
