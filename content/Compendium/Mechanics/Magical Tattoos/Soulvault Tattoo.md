**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: Any Torso
- Level 2: Any Head

*This tattoo is special and requires special circumstances and knowledge to apply. (Which is to say this is a lore thing not something you can just get lmao)*

#### Soulvault Tattoo (I)
**Soulvault.** This tattoo stores the soul of a creature, using the bearer of the tattoo's lifeforce to sustain the Vaulted Soul. This level only stores the soul in metaphysical stasis. Powerful souls may be able to influence their host.

#### Soulvault (II)
**Expanded Soulvault.** The expanded tattoo allows the Vaulted Soul to experience consciousness while it's host is unconscious. While this does not give them control of the shared body, it may influence the host's dreams or even impress on their subconscious when the body is in a compromised state.

**Protections or Compromises.** The expansion of the tattoo can impart protections for the host, limiting the Vaulted Soul's influence over the body, or compromise the host's autonomy allowing the Vaulted Soul more influence.
