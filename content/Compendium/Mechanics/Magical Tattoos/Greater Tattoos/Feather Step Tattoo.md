**Body Part Requirements:**
- Both Legs

#### Featherstep
**Silencing Ink.** As a [[Magic]] Action, your footsteps no longer make sound or imprints for 1 hour. You may end the effect early as a free action. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

**Impact Ink.** You take half damage from falling as long as you are not [[Unconscious]] or [[Restrained]]. Additionally, per the [[Falling]] rules, if you jump willingly, you can fall twice as far before fall damage starts incrementing.
