**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: One Torso
- Level 2: One Torso + Any Body Part
- Level 3: One Torso + Any two Body Parts

#### Barrier (I)
**Tattoo Armor.** While you aren't wearing armor, the tattoo grants you an [[Armor Class]] of 12 + your Dexterity modifier.

#### Barrier (II)
**Tattoo Armor.** While you aren't wearing armor, the tattoo grants you an [[Armor Class]] of 15 + your Dexterity modifier (maximum of +2).

#### Barrier (III)
**Tattoo Armor.** While you aren't wearing armor, the tattoo grants you an [[Armor Class]] of 18 + your Dexterity modifier (maximum of +2).
