**Body Part Requirements:**
- Scalp

#### Watchward
**Aura Suppression.** [[Detect Magic]] and similar effects does not reveal any magic worn or carried by you unless observed from 10 feet away or less and the magical school of observed magic is obscured.

**Scry Shield.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against [[Scrying]] spells.

**Seer Sense.** You know when any kind of divination magic or [[Truesight]] is being used to observe you.

**Shrouded Mind.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against being Charmed or effects that would deal Psychic damage.
