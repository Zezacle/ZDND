#### Spellwrought Tattoo
**Spellskin.** During the tattoo's application, a creature within 5 feet can expend a spell slot to cast a single spell of up to 5th level. Instead of manifesting, the spell's magic is inscribed and stored within the skin. The bearer of the tattoo can cast the stored spell, requiring no material components. The spell uses the properties listed in the table below based on the spell's level. The tattoo glows faintly while you cast the spell and for the spell's duration. Once the spell ends, the tattoo vanishes from your skin.

| Spell Level | Spellcasting <br>Ability Modifier | Save DC | Attack Bonus |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | ------- | ------------ |
| Cantrip     | +3                                | 13      | +5           |
| 1st         | +3                                | 13      | +5           |
| 2nd         | +3                                | 13      | +5           |
| 3rd         | +4                                | 15      | +7           |
| 4th         | +4                                | 15      | +7           |
| 5th         | +5                                | 17      | +9           |

**No Attunement.** This tattoo does not use an [[Attunement]] slot.
