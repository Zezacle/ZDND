**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: One Torso
- Level 2: One Torso + Any Body Part
- Level 3: One Torso + Any two Body Parts

#### Lifewell (I)
**Life Transfer.** As an Action, draw life from your body out of the tattoo and transfer it into another creature. Roll 1d10. You take an amount of damage equal to the roll. This damage ignores any [[Temporary Hit Points]] you may have. 

A creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet heals an amount of [[Hit Points]] equal to the roll.

#### Lifewell (II)
**Greater Life Transfer.** When you use the Life Transfer feature, you can do it twice as part of one Action, targeting the same or different creatures. Additionally, the damage you take per Life Transfer performed is reduced by your Constitution modifier, if it is positive.

#### Lifewell (III)
**Amplified Life Transfer.** When you use the Life Transfer feature, you can do it three times as part of one Action, targeting the same or different creatures. Additionally, the affected creatures gain additional health equal to their Constitution modifier (minimum 0) per Life Transfer they receive.

*Per Amplified Life Transfer (uses the same 1d10 roll):*
- *Damage you take: 1d10 - your Con Mod (if positive)*
- *Health gained by target: 1d10 + their Con Mod (if positive)*
