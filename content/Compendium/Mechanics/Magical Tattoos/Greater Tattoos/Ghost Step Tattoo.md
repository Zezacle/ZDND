**Body Part Requirements:**
- Both Legs + Any Torso

#### Ghost Step Tattoo
**Ghostly Form.** The tattoo has 3 charges, and it regains all expended charges at [[Holy Dawn]].

As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 of the tattoo's charges to become incorporeal until the end of your next turn. For the duration, you gain the following benefits:
- You have [[Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
- You can't be [[Grappled]] or [[Restrained]].
- You can move through creatures and solid objects as if they were [[Difficult Terrain]]. If the effect ends while you are inside a solid object, you are shunted to the nearest unoccupied space, and you take 1d10 Force damage for every 5 feet traveled.
