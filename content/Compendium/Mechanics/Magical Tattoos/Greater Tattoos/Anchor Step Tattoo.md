**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: One Leg
- Level 2: Both Legs

#### Anchor Step (I)
**Anchored Foot.** As a Bonus Action, you can empower the tattoo for 1 minute. For this duration, your [[Speed]] is 0, you are immune to forced movement (except gravity). You can end the effect early as a free action. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

**Weighted Ink.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against forced movement.

#### Anchor Step (II)
**Anchored Feet.** As a Bonus Action, you can empower the tattoo for 1 minute. For this duration, you have the effects of the [[Spider Climb]] spell. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

**Dimensional Anchor.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against effects that would cause you to teleport or change dimensions against your will. This does not apply to portals that you fall into.

**Immoveable Ink.** You are immune to forced movement (except gravity).
