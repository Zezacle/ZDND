**Body Part Requirements:**
- Level 1: Any Body Part
- Level 2: Any two Body Parts

#### Absorbing (I)
**Damage Resistance.** Choose a damage type from the table or roll for a random one. While the tattoo is on your skin, you have [[Resistance]] to that damage type.

#### Absorbing (II)
**Damage Resistance.** Choose an additional damage type from the table or roll for a random one (reroll if the same as previous). While the tattoo is on your skin, you have [[Resistance]] to that damage type.

**Damage Absorption.** When you take damage of either chosen type, you can use your Reaction to gain [[Immunity]] against that instance of the damage, and you regain a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to half the damage you would have taken. Once this Reaction is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
