**Body Part Requirements:**
- Scalp

#### Spellmind
**Focusing Ink.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws made to maintain [[Concentration]] on spells casted with a 3rd level spell slot or lower.

**Second Channel.** You do not need to maintain [[Concentration]] on spells casted with a 1st level spell slot or lower.

**Spell Well.** When you complete a [[Short Rest]], you regain a number of 1st level spell slots equal to your half your proficiency bonus (rounded down).

*WARNING: This tattoo is not currently written to work with spells upcasted through Warlock's Pact Slots.*
