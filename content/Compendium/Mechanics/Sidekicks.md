Whether hired, recruited, or stuck with, a party may gain Sidekicks.

A sidekick is an NPC added to the party that is player-controlled during Combat. Sidekicks grow in power with the PCs, expanding their abilities. Additionally, like PCs, if reduced to 0 [[Hit Points]], they roll [[Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]], following the same rules as PCs.

### Combat Initiative
If all PCs and the Sidekick are present at the call for the Sidekick's [[Initiative]], a die is rolled to determine who will control the Sidekick (eg. 1d5 for 5 players). Whichever player is selected, the Sidekick is given the same Initiative count and placed after them in the Initiative order.

A player may abdicate their control over the Sidekick to another player or the DM. If Combat has already begun when this happens, the Sidekick does not move in the turn order.

#### Absent PC
If a PC is absent from a Combat Encounter, but the Sidekick is present, the player who's PC is absent takes control, rolling the Sidekick's [[Initiative]] as a normal PC would.

#### Turn Concurrency
If the Sidekick and a PC have the same [[Initiative]] count, the Sidekick's turn is concurrent with the player allowing the PC and Sidekick to act at the same time (ex. PC uses action, Sidekick uses Bonus Action, then they both move, then PC uses Bonus Action).
