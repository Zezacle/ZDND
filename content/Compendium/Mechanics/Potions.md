Potions are consumable elixirs that provide beneficial magic effects. 

### Consumption
You can consume a potion as a Bonus Action, or administer it to an unconscious creature as an Action.

#### Healing Potions
If the potion provides [[Hit Points]] or [[Temporary Hit Points]] you may choose to consume it as an Action, and receive the maximum potential healing and/or Temp HP effect of the potion.

## Potion Sickness & Toxicity (Optional Rule)
Consuming multiple potions in a short period of time may induce Potion Sickness. The stronger or lower quality a potion is, the higher its potential to induce sickness.

### Potion Toxicity
Each potion has a Toxicity value, ranging from 1 to 8, as specified in its description. When a potion is consumed, you must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure your Toxicity level increases by the potion's Toxicity value, or half as much (rounded down) on a success.

Toxicity level decreases by the creature's Constitution modifier every 4 hours. Toxicity can be reduced by consuming a [[Potion of Purging]] or removed entirely by a [[Potion of Vitality]] or [[Lesser Restoration]] spell.

#### General Toxicity by Potion Rarity
A potion's Toxicity usually follows its rarity, however some potions have higher or lower toxicities, such as [[Potion of Healing|Potions of Healing]].

| Potion Rarity | Toxicity |
| ------------- | -------- |
| Common        | 1        |
| Uncommon      | 2        |
| Rare          | 4        |
| Very Rare     | 6        |
| Legendary     | 8        |
#### Potion Toxicity Modifiers
Some potions may have additional toxicity modifiers based on the potion's quality. These modifiers can stack. Negative modifiers can be removed one at a time using a [[Purify Food and Drink]] spell. A potion under the effects of 

| Toxicity | Modifier       |
| -------- | -------------- |
| +1       | Old            |
| +2       | Poorly Quality |
| -2       | Warded         |
For example, an **Old and Poor Quality** [[Potion of Climbing]] would have a Toxicity value of 4 and a **Warded** [[Potion of Clairvoyance]] would have a Toxicity value of 2.

### Potion Sickness
Potion Sickness has multiple levels of severity. When a creature's Toxicity level is above certain thresholds, they are subjected to all Potion Sickness effects of that Toxicity level and below.

| Toxicity Level | Effects                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5              | You have an additional level of [[Exhaustion]] until your Toxicity level is below 5.                                                                                   |
| 7              | You have the [[Poisoned]] condition until your Toxicity level is below 7.                                                                                              |
| 9              | You have an additional level of [[Exhaustion]] until your Toxicity level is below 9.                                                                                   |
| 11             | Every 4 hours you must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC equal to your Toxicity level. On a failure, you take 3d8 Poison damage or half as much on a success. |
