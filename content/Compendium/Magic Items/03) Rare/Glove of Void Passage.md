*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Attunement:** Yes
**Stored Spell:** [[Void Passage]]
**Charges Left:** 1 per [[Holy Dawn]]
> [!checks|no-title clean]
> -
>  - [ ] %% %%

While wearing the [[Glove of Void Passage]], you have the [[Void Passage]] spell prepared and can cast it with the appropriate level spell slots.

You can cast [[Void Passage]] once as a Bonus Action without using a spell slot and instead expending the glove's charge.

*This leather glove is stained purple and purple-colored static electricity can be seen arcing between the glove's material as the fingers rub together while worn.*
