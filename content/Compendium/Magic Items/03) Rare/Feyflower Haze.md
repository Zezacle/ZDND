*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 200 GP
**Weight:** 0.5 lb
**Uses:** Single Use

*A purple powder extracted from a Feyflower. A sweet, if overpowering scent betrays its enchanting effects.*

**Mind Haze.** As an Action, blow the powder towards a target you can see within 30 ft. The powder drifts toward them on an invisible breeze before surrounding their head. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or begin hallucinating for 1 minute. While under the effects of the Haze, they can only interact with their hallucination on their turn. If touched or attacked while under the effects of the Haze, whoever interacted with them is included in their hallucination and the target can take actions with or against them. 

On their turn, the target can spend their Action to roll the saving throw again, ending the effect on a success.
