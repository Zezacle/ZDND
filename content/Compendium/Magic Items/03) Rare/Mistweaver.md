*Weapon ([[Recurve Bow]]), Rare*

**Attunement:** One Slot
**Cost:** 500 GP
**Weight:** 2 lb.
**Uses:** 1 charge, recharges at Dusk

*This ornately crafted [[Recurve Bow]] bears engravings of the Feywild and emits a thin veil of mist.*

**Masterwork Weapon.** This weapon grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it.

**Mist Affinity.** While attuned to this item, you always have the [[Misty Step]] spell prepared and can cast it using a charge.

**Misted Arrow.** As an attack, you can consume a charge to conjure and fire a special arrow made of mist. The arrow travels up to 60 feet, creating a curtain of mist in its trail before dissipating. This curtain extends downward to the ground, and [[Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscures]] anything perceived through it.

  Creatures that attempt to pass through the curtain must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw equal to your Spell Save DC or become [[Blinded]] until the start of their next turn. You can allow any creature to automatically succeed this save.

**Misty Shot.** As an Action, you can consume a charge to touch a willing creature and then choose a point within 300 ft. that you could theoretically hit with an arrow. The creature will be teleported to wherever the arrow lands. 

  Make a ranged attack roll (at [[Disadvantage]] if beyond 60 ft.) If the result is over 15, you hit your intended target. Below 15, and the target is displaced 5 ft. for every 1 under 15.
