*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Attunement:** Yes
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Once per dusk

*A wooden mask of a raven's beak painted red. Worn by Hodd, Odin's Treasure Raven.*

**Blessed Eyes.** While wearing the mask, you can read and understand Celestial.

**Hodd's Form.** Once per dusk, as a [[Magic]] action while wearing the mask, you [[Polymorph]] in a Raven for 10 minutes.

**Treasure Seeker.** By donning the mask, you can cast the [[Lost and Found]] spell at 1st level without material components. The spirit that guides you to treasure resembles a green raven. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until dusk.
