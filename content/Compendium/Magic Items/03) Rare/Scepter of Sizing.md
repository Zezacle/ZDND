*Rod, Rare*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 500 GP
**Weight:** 4 lb.
**Uses:** 3, recharge on [[Holy Dawn]]

*A black scepter with a blue gem floating at the end. The gem grows and shrinks in size at a slow pace.*

**Enlarge / Reduce.** The wielder of this scepter can cast [[Enlarge + Reduce]] as a [[Magic]] action, consuming a charge. The DC to save against this effect is 15 or the caster's Spell Save DC, whichever is higher.

**Perfect Size.** As a [[Magic]] action, the wielder can focus on a creature within 10-feet, and point the scepter at a garment or piece of [[Armor]] that isn't being worn or carried. The object reshapes to fit the creature's anatomy so that it can be worn by that creature.
