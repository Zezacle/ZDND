*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 75 GP
**Weight:** 2 lb.
**Uses:** N/A

*A hooded lantern with a everburning flame inside.*

The flame inside the lantern casts [[Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. It looks like a white flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched. The flame can only be put out if it is targeted by [[Dispel Magic]], enters an [[Antimagic Field]] or the lantern is destroyed.

As an [[Utilize]] action, you can lower the lantern's hood, reducing the light to [[Dim Light]] in a 5-foot radius.
