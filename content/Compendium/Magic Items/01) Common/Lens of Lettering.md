*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** -
**Uses:** -

*A circular 1-inch lens in a brass frame. There are micro-etchings in both the glass and frame of many different scripts.*

**Translating Trinket.** When pressed upon writing, the writing appears translated into Common when viewed through the lens. The lens does nothing if the font does not fit entirely within the Lens' diameter.
