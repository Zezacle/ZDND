_Potion, Common_

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Single Use

**Toxicity:** 1

*This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors.*

**Climbing.** When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Climbing]] Speed equal to your [[Speed]] for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[Advantage]] on [[Athletics]] checks to climb.
