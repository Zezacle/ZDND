*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A rounded, porous and cursed river stone the size of fist.*

**Unnatural Thirst.** The stone absorbs water and humidity it comes into contact with at a rate of 1 gallon per minute, up to 250 gallons per day.
