_Potion, Common_

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Single Use

**Toxicity:** 1

*This potion is an unsettling yellow-green and tastes overtly tart and sweet. Some believe it to be addictive. Others say they can stop whenever they want.*

**Energy.** When you drink this potion, it removes one level of [[Exhaustion]]. Additionally, you have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against [[Exhaustion]] for 4 hours.
