*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A porous volcanic stone that can store up to 20 seconds of audio within the metal rune pressed into it's surface.*

**Recording.** As an [[Actions|Action]], press the brass rune on the stone to begin recording audio near to the stone. The stone will record and store up to 20 seconds of audio. The stone can clearly record normal volume speech up to 30-feet away or whispers up to 5-feet away. To replay stored audio, simply rub the face of the rune again. The stone can be activated at a distance with [[Mage Hand]]. Creatures must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom ([[Insight]]) Check to determine the audio is a recording if they cannot see the stone.

To clear stored audio, simply cast [[Message]], [[Mending]], or [[Prestidigitation]] on the stone.
