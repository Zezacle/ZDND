_Wondrous Item, Common_

**Attunement:** Required ([[Warlock]] only)
**Cost:** 75 GP
**Weight:** 0.5 lb.
**Uses:** Once per [[Long Rest]]

*This amulet is fashioned from a shard of resilient material originating from an otherworldly realm.*

**Spellcasting Focus.** You can use the amulet as a Spellcasting Focus for your [[Warlock]] spells.

**Unknown Spell.** As a [[Magic]] action, you can try to cast a cantrip that you don't know. The cantrip must be on the [[Warlock Spell List.base|Warlock Spell List]] and have a casting time of an Action, and you make a DC 10 Intelligence ([[Arcana]]) check. On a successful check, you cast the spell. On a failed check, the spell fails, and the action used to cast it is wasted. In either case, you can't use this property again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].
