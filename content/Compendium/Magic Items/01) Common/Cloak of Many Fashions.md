_Wondrous Item, Common_

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 75 GP
**Weight:** 2 lb.
**Uses:** -

*When not worn, the dye of this cloak slowly changes color throughout the day.*

**Shapeshifting Fabric.** While wearing this cloak, you can take a Bonus Action to change the style, color, and apparent quality of the garment. The cloak's weight doesn't change. Regardless of its appearance, the cloak can't be anything but a cloak. Although it can duplicate the appearance of other magic cloaks, it doesn't gain their magical properties.
