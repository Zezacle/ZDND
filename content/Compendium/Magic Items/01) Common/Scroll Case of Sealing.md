*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 75 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Single Use

*An enchanted leather scroll case capable of keeping its contents in pristine condition.*

**Sealing.** Once latched closed, no dirt, water, debris or air can be exchanged from the interior or exterior of the case without the deliberate unlatching of the case's lid.

Additionally, if an [[Alarm]] spell is casted on this item, it stays indefinitely and telepathically notifies the caster of the case's opening regardless of distance, as long as they are on the same plane of existence. This effect can be negated with [[Dispel Magic]] or other similar effect.
