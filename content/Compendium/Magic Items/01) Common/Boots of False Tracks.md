*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 50 GP
**Weight:** 2 lb.
**Uses:** -

*When not worn, the treads of these boots change when not observed.*

**False Tracks.** While wearing these boots, you can have them leave tracks like those of any kind of Humanoid of your size.
