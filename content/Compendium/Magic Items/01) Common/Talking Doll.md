*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Attunement:** Yes
**Cost:** 30 GP
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A small cloth doll stuffed with sheep's wool and wonder.*

**Fabric Friend.** While this doll is within 5-feet of you, you can spend an hour telling it to say up to six phrases, none of which can be more than six words long, and set a condition under which the doll speaks each phrase. You can also replace old phrases with new ones. Whatever the condition, it must occur within 5-feet of the doll to make it speak. For example, whenever someone picks up the doll, it might say, "I want a piece of candy." The doll's phrases are lost when your Attunement to the doll ends.
