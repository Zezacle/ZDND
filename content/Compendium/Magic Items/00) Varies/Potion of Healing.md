*Potion, Varies*

**Toxicity:** 1

You regain [[Hit Points]] when you drink this potion. The number of [[Hit Points]] depends on the potion's rarity, as shown in the table below.

If you consume this potion as an action, you regain the maximum potential HP.

*Whatever its potency, the potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated.*

| Rarity    | Potion                       | HP Regained | Max Potential |
| --------- | ---------------------------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| Common    | Potion of Healing (Lesser)   | 2d4 + 2     | 10            |
| Uncommon  | Potion of Healing (Greater)  | 4d4 + 4     | 20            |
| Rare      | Potion of Healing (Superior) | 8d4 + 8     | 40            |
| Very Rare | Potion of Healing (Supreme)  | 10d4 + 20   | 60            |
