*Wondrous Item, Legendary*

**Attunement:** Yes, [[Wizard]] only
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Once per dusk

*A tome bound in black dragonskin with platinum bracings and lock. Only the attuned can magically produce the key to unlock the tome. The pages within are made with archaic, untidy parchment. The first pages are written in a old dialect of [[Draconic]], detailing an account of the aftermath of Ragnarok. The rest of the tome's many pages are now filled with a perfect copy of the Attuner's known spells in their handwriting, written in [[Draconic]].*

**Draconic Memory.** Taking 4 hours you can focus on the pages, morphing the written spells into others once recorded in the tome. You can change up to 4 of your known spells to other spells in the [[Wizard Spell List]] of a level for which you have spell slots for.

After doing this, you must make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 11 + # of spells changed) or suffer a level of [[Exhaustion]].

**Spellbound Tome.** This item acts a [[Locking Tome]] [[Spellbook]] and Spellcasting Focus for a [[Wizard]].

**Ward of the Dragon.** If any creature tries to pick the tome's lock (DC 18) and fails, they take 4d6 fire damage. Additionally, the pages of the tome cannot be torn out and seem to duplicate and expand the bindings of the tome as needed.

**Writ Magic.** You learn three additional cantrips from the [[Wizard Spell List]].
