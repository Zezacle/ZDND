*Wondrous Item, Legendary*

**Attunement:** Yes, [[Wizard]] only
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** 1 lb.
**Uses:** Once per dusk

*A tome bound in black dragonskin with platinum bracings and lock. Only the attuned can magically produce the key to unlock the tome. The pages within are made with archaic, untidy parchment. The first pages are written in a old dialect of [[Draconic]], detailing an account of the aftermath of Ragnarok. The rest of the tome's 100 pages are seemingly blank.*

**Spellbound Tome.** This item acts a [[Locking Tome]] [[Spellbook]] and Spellcasting Focus for a [[Wizard]].

**Draco Spells.** The wielder of this item learns the [[Armor of Rimefrost]], [[Dragon's Breath]], and [[Ring of Fire]] spells. Additionally, one of these spells can be casted without using a spell slot. When a spell is casted this way, it is casted as a 2nd level spell. Once this feature is used, it cannot be used again until dusk.

**Ward of the Dragon.** If any creature tries to pick the tome's lock (DC 18) and fails, they take 4d6 fire damage. Additionally, unattuned creature viewing the pages see the written text as a shifting amalgamation of symbols and ink.
