*Wondrous Item, Legendary*

**Attunement:** Automatic, no slot consumed
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** -
**Uses:** -

*An orb made to focus and harness the foresight of the True Giants of old.*

**Giant's Foresight.** Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest]], roll three d20s and record the numbers rolled. You can replace any [[D20 Test]] made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You can replace a roll in this way only once per turn. Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a [[Long Rest]], you lose any unused foretelling rolls.

**Mystic Visions.** Like Deja Vu, glimpses of the future begin to press on your awareness.

**Harold of Futures.** You can cast [[Divination]] as a ritual using the Jotun's Eye once per [[Holy Dawn]].

**Tongue of Time.** You learn [[Giant]]. You can telepathically speak to a creature you can see, but only in [[Giant]]. They hear your words as whispers in their mind.
