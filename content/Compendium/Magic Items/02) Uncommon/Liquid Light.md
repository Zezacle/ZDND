*A clear flask of white liquid that shimmers with a chromatic film bound in a twine harness. When uncorked, part of the liquid quickly evaporates into a gas, and the reaction causes the remaining liquid to glow intensely. Adding a few ounces of water to the flask will trigger the liquid regenerating itself over the next 12 hours.*
