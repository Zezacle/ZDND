*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** Yes
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** 0.5 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A gold chain with a chunk of gold ore as an amulet. Or is it?*

**Curse of Fool's Gold.** This item is cursed. Upon attuning to this item, the wearer is granted 1,000 GP and can no longer take off the Amulet by any means. Attuning to it curses you until you have 2,000 GP more than when you attuned to it, at which point the Amulet consumes the gold and the curse ends leaving the Amulet inert. If the curse is ended before this by the [[Remove Curse]] spell, the Amulet automatically consumes the nearest 1,000 GP and becomes inert.
