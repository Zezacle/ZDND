*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** Required, one slot

This wood chisel brims with a magic energy capable of infusing animated life into its carvings. Additionally, it doubles as an entire set of [[Woodcarver's Tools]].

With 4 hours of work and a piece of wood, you can carve a wooden effigy of a Tiny or smaller creature. Upon completion, the effigy awakens and has the **Wooden Critter** stat block (see below). 

The creature is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the creature shares your [[Initiative]] count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the [[Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.

The construct stays awakened for up to 7 days or until you carve a new one or its [[Hit Points]] reach 0. When any of these conditions are met, the construct turns to sawdust and wood chips.
