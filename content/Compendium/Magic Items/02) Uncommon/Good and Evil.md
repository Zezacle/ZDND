*Weapon (Pair of [[Knuckles]]), Uncommon*

*A pair of knuckle dusters. One glows dimly, carved of white marble with gold plated reinforcements. The other, carved of basalt, has a reddish silver inlayed into the cracks. For some reason, they are magically tethered together in a way that makes it impossible to separate them by more than a few inches unless they are worn by one person.*

#### Good
*Weapon ([[Knuckles]])*

**Mastery:** [[Vex]] 
**Weight:** 0.5 lb.
**Damage:** 1d4 Radiant
**Properties:** [[Finesse]], [[Light]]

**Stored Spell:** Cast 1st Level [[Divine Smite]] without using a spell slot.
**Charges Left:** Recharges at [[Holy Dawn]]

#### Evil
*Weapon ([[Knuckles]])*

**Mastery:** [[Sap]] 
**Weight:** 0.5 lb.
**Damage:** 1d4 Necrotic
**Properties:** [[Finesse]], [[Light]]

**Stored Spell:** Cast 1st Level [[Inflict Wounds]] as a Bonus Action on hit without using a spell slot.
**Charges Left:** Recharges at [[Holy Dawn]]
> [!checks|no-title clean]
> -
>  - [ ] %% %%

[[Good and Evil]] both register to the [[Detect Good and Evil]] spell, and creatures under the effect of [[Protection from Good and Evil]] take bludgeoning damage instead of Radiant or Necrotic damage when hit by [[Good and Evil]].

Additionally, strikes made with [[Good and Evil]] can benefit from effects that apply to bludgeoning strikes such as the [[Crusher]] feat's **Push** effect.
