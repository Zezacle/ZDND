*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** Yes
**Cost:** 200 GP
**Weight:** 4 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A brown cloak that glows in a blue trim when the wearer is concentrating on something. If they concentrate harder, they can hide the glow.*

**Focus Shield.** While [[Attunement|Attuned]] and wearing this cloak, you have [[Advantage]] on [[Concentration]] checks. If you fail a [[Concentration]] check, you cannot use this feature again until Dusk.

**Focus Glow.** The cloak can be used to emit 5-feet of [[Dim Light]] by concentrating on a task.
