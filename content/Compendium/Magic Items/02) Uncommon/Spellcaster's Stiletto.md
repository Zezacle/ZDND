*Weapon ([[Dagger]]), Uncommon*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 150 GP
**Weight:** -
**Uses:** -

*A silver stiletto dagger in a simple leather sheath. Engravings in it's narrow blade allow the channeling of spells through it.*

**Silvered.** This weapon deals Force damage to creatures with the ability to Shape-Shift.

**Spellcasting Focus.** This weapon can be used as a Spellcasting Focus for Artificers, Sorcerers and Wizards.

**Stiletto Dagger.** This weapon ignores bonuses to a target's [[Armor Class]] from worn armor, excluding [[Shields]] and Natural Armor.

**Unbalanced.** his dagger does not have the [[Thrown]] property.
