*Potion, Uncommon*

When consumed, a creature can gain the benefits of a rest over an 8 hour period without sleeping as long as they do not exert themselves excessively.

*This potion has a small creature's eye floating in a yellowish-green bubbling liquid. It tastes sweet and sour, and the eye gives it a crunch!*
