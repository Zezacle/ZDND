*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Charges Left:** Recharges at [[Holy Dawn]]

As an Action, you can blow this horn and choose any single creature to hear its sound. No other creature hears the horn, and the targeted creature knows the sound is meant for them. A creature can be targeted from any range within the same plane of existence, however the effect differs based on the distance. The horn has no effect on creatures that have no way to perceive sound, but does work on creatures that usually can, but are currently [[Deafened]].

If the creature is within 60 feet, the sound is deafeningly loud to them. If you can see the creature, they must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 2d8 Psychic damage and is [[Deafened]] for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

If the creature is beyond 60 feet away, it hears the horn loud and clearly.

If the creature is beyond 1 mile away, the horn's sound appears softly in the distance. They only notice the sound if their [[Passive Perception]] is higher than 12.
