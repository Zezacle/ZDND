*Potion, Uncommon*

**Toxicity:** 4

When you drink this potion, you have [[Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage for 1 hour.

*This potion's liquid appears grey in color until agitated, at which point the liquid seems crystalizes into a stone-like texture on the walls of the glass until reverting.*
