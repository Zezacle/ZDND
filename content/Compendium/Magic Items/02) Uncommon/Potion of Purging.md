*Potion, Uncommon*

**Toxicity:** 0

Upon consumption, all active potion effects affecting the creature end. If the creature is under the effects of a [[Poison]], it may make a Constitution saving throw with [[Advantage]] and end the poison's effects on a success.

If the creature has a Toxicity level, it is reduced by 1d6 + your Constitution modifier.

*This potion resembles water clouded with a white tint and has a sweet and sour candy-like flavor.*
