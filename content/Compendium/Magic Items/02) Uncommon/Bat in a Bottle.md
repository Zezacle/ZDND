*Wondrous Item (Consumable, Uncommon)*

When the [[Bat in a Bottle]] is thrown it shatters on impact releasing a screech. Whomever threw the bottle perceives the area around the impact site in a 20-foot-radius for 6 seconds as if they were standing there and had [[Blindsight]].

*A jar filled with a yellow liquid with a preserved bat's head floating in the center. The head blinks it's clouded eyes periodically.*
