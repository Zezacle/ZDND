*Potion, Uncommon*

**Toxicity:** 2

When you drink this potion, you have [[Resistance]] to Cold damage for 1 hour.

*This potion's liquid appears grey in color until agitated, at which point the liquid fades a bluish white and frosts the glass until settling again.*
