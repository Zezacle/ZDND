*Potion, Uncommon*

**Toxicity:** 2

When you drink this potion, you have [[Resistance]] to Radiant damage for 1 hour.

*This potion's liquid appears grey in color until agitated, at which point the liquid fades yellow and a white light flickers in the center until settling again.*
