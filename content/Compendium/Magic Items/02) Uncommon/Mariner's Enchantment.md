*Enchantment (Armor), Uncommon*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** Base Item Cost + 150 GP
**Weight:** -
**Uses:** -

*The armor is decorated with fish and shell motifs. If old or disused, barnacles grow on the armor's surface.*

**Mariner.** While wearing this armor, you have a [[Swimming]] Speed equal to your [[Speed]]. In addition, if you start your turn underwater with 0 [[Hit Points]], you immediately regain 1d4 [[Hit Points]]. The armor can't heal anyone again until the next Dusk.
