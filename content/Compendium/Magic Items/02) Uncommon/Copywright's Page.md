*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** No
**Cost:** 150 GP (comes in pack of 2)
**Weight:** -
**Uses:** Single Use

*A enchanted and gold-embossed pair of parchment sheets. When one is marked, the other shares the mark. These pages are used to create duplicates of documents or share messages.*

**Magical Link.** When either of the paired pages are marked with ink, charcoal, blood, or other kind of writing substance, an illusory copy appears on the other page. The link updates in real-time if the pages are within 100-feet of each other, otherwise they update at dawn. The link is suspended if the pages are not on the same plane of existence. 

The link exists for 7 days, after which it is broken and the pages no longer update to match each other. The link is also broken if either page is torn, soiled, destroyed or the magic is dispelled (via [[Dispel Magic]]). Visually, a broken link is indicated by the golden embossed border appearing to tarnish.
