*Crafting Supply, Uncommon*

**Cost:** 250 GP
**Weight:** 0.5 lb.

A sealed vial of enchanted ink designed to be tattooed into skin to elicit magical effects. One vial is enough to tattoo one body part.

*See [[Magical Tattoos]].*

**Crafting Recipe.** This item can be crafted by mixing at least 200 GP worth of dust made from Gemstones of Rare or rarer rarity.
