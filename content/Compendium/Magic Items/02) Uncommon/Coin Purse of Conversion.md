*Wondrous Item (Container), Uncommon*

**Weight:** 1 lb.

This simple looking leather coin purse is imbued with powerful transmutation magic via runes and enchanted leather.

Inside is a pocket dimension that accepts only currency coinage. Coins placed into this pouch are automatically converted into a nebulous state of currency. The purse can hold up to 50,000 GP (or 100 lb. of Platinum pieces). When you reach into the purse to withdraw currency, you somehow always pull out the exact amount you intend to in the exact denominations you wish.

If the coin purse is overloaded, pierced, or torn, it ruptures and is destroyed, and its contents are scattered in the Astral Plane. If the coin purse is turned inside out, its contents spill forth, unharmed as platinum pieces and any remaining change, but the coin purse must be put right before it can be used again.

Placing a [[Coin Purse of Conversion]] inside an extradimensional space created by a [[Bag of Holding]], [[Handy Haversack]], [[Portable Hole]], or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates where the one item was placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 feet of the gate is sucked through it to a random location on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. The gate is one-way only and can't be reopened.
