*Wondrous Item (Consumable), Uncommon*

When a creature eats a [[Hag's Eye]], they gain the benefits of the [[Arcane Eye]] spell without needing [[Concentration]].

Additionally, the creature must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have a level of [[Exhaustion]] for 1 hour.

*This yellowed eye has a dark cloudy iris and the smell of sulfur. The eye stalk still squirms when the eye is agitated.*
