*Potion, Very Rare*

**Toxicity:** 6

When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Flying]] Speed equal to your Speed for 1 hour and can hover. If you're in the air when the potion wears off, you fall unless you have some other means of staying aloft.

*This potion's clear liquid floats at the top of its container and has cloudy white impurities drifting in it.*
