Barbarians who follow the Path of the Voice channel ancient magic through phrases of ancient languages known as a Shout, the knowledge of which is gifted to them through mystical means. Shouts can invoke a variety of effects, from pure magical force to command over another's mind.

*Inspired by TES V: Skyrim by Bethesda Softworks.* 
*Original 5e adaptation by Ginger2996.*

### Level 3: Voice Within

You are able to channel magic through your voice to produce powerful effects.

**Using a Shout.** As a Bonus Action you can perform a Shout and expend a use. Similar to some spellcasting, all Shouts require a Verbal component, and therefore cannot be used if you cannot speak (eg. while affected by the [[Silence]] spell). Additionally, your Shout can be heard up to 150 feet away.

**Number of Uses.** You can perform shouts a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of once) and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Number of Shouts Known.** You know a number of Shouts equal to your Proficiency Bonus. When you complete a [[Long Rest]], you can change one of your known shouts.

**Shout Save DC.** The DC to avoid the effects of your shouts is 8 + your Constitution modifier + Proficiency bonus.

### Level 3: Knowledge of the Voice

You learn the [[Thaumaturgy]] cantrip.

### Level 3: Voice of Power

You learn the **Unrelenting Force** shout, which does not count against the number of shouts you can know at once.

While raging, you can perform the Unrelenting Force shout once per Rage without expending a use of your Shouts.

### Level 6: Raging Voice

When you use a bonus action to Rage, you can expend a use of your shouts to perform a Shout as part of the same bonus action.

### Level 10: Expanded Voice

You can now use the **Shout Mastery** effect of one of your known shouts. If you know a Shout's Mastery, you cannot change that Shout for another known shout at the end of a [[Long Rest]].

You can change your known **Shout Mastery** by meditating uninterrupted for 4 hours.

### Level 14: Master of the Voice

You can now use the **Shout Mastery** for one additional known shout. Meditating to change a Shout Mastery only takes 1 hour.

You know the **Shout Mastery** for Unrelenting Force and it does not count against your known Shout Masteries.

## Shouts

#### Battlefury
*(Mid Vur Shaan)*

Choose a number of creatures equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of one) within 30 feet of you that you can see, and grant them [[Advantage]] on their next attack roll. You can include yourself as one of the creatures.

This shout has no effect on creatures who cannot hear you such as [[Deafened]] creatures.

**Shout Mastery.** The range increases by 10 feet and targeted creatures gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Constitution modifier + your Proficiency bonus.


#### Bend Will
*(Gol Hah Dov)*

One Humanoid you can see within 30 feet makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the [[Charmed]] condition for 1 minute or until you or your allies damage it. The [[Charmed]] creature is Friendly to you. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the [[Charmed]] condition on a success.

When the effect ends, the target knows it was [[Charmed]] by you.

This shout has no effect on creatures who cannot hear you such as [[Deafened]] creatures.

**Shout Mastery.** You can target any creature type with this shout.

#### Breathe Fire
*(Yol Toor Shul)*

Each creature in a 30-foot [[Cone]] in front of you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes Fire damage equal to 1d6 times your Constitution modifier, or half as much on a success.

**Shout Mastery.** The damage die for this shout is a d8 and the range increases 10 feet.

#### Breathe Frost
*(Fo Krah Diin)*

Each creature in a 30-foot [[Cone]] in front of you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes Cold damage equal to 1d6 times your Constitution modifier, or half as much on a success.

**Shout Mastery.** The damage die for this shout is a d8 and the range increases 10 feet.

#### Disarm
*(Zun Haal Viik)*

Creatures in a 30-foot [[Cone]] in front of you must succeed on a Strength saving throw or drop what they are holding, with the object landing in its space. The target has [[Advantage]] on their saving throw if they are holding an item with two hands.

**Shout Mastery.** Creatures do not get Advantage for holding an item with two hands and successfully disarming a creature of an object results in the object being pushed up to 20 feet behind them.

#### Dismay
*(Faas Ru Maar)*

Hostile creatures within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Frightened]] condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the [[Frightened]] condition on a success.

This shout has no effect on creatures who cannot hear you such as [[Deafened]] creatures.

**Shout Mastery.** Attack rolls against the [[Frightened]] creatures have [[Advantage]].

#### Storm
*(Strun Bah Qo)*

You conjure storm clouds to strike your foes with lighting. At the end of your next turn, any hostile creature within 15 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 2d8 Lightning damage or half as much on a success.

**Shout Mastery.** The range increases 15 feet and the damage increases to 4d8 Lightning damage.

#### Unrelenting Force
*(Fus Ro Dah)*

Creatures in a 30-foot [[Cone]] in front of you must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed back 15 feet. Large or larger creatures have [[Advantage]] on their saving throw.

If the targets' forced movement is interrupted, they take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 5 feet not travelled. If the interruption was another creature, both creatures take half damage.

**Shout Mastery.** This shout deals 2d6 Thunder damage or half as much on a successful save and pushes targets 30 feet.

#### Whirlwind
*(Wuld Nah Kest)*

You rush forward faster than the eye can track. Move through unoccupied spaces in a straight line up to 30 feet. This movement does not provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]]. This movement can only be used to move horizontally, but is not affected by gravity, and therefore could be used to cross gaps.

**Shout Mastery.** Creatures are within 5 feet of you as you pass by must make a Constitution saving throw. On a fail, they take 1d8 Thunder damage or half as much on a success.
