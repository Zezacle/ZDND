The Oath of the Crown binds Paladins to the ideals of civilization, be it the spirit of a nation, fealty to a sovereign, or service to a deity of law and rulership. The paladins who swear this oath dedicate themselves to serving society and, in particular, the just laws that hold society together. 

These paladins are the watchful guardians on the walls, standing against the chaotic tides of barbarism that threaten to tear down all that civilization has built, and are commonly known as guardians, exemplars, or sentinels.

These Paladins often share the following tenets:
- Uphold and respect the laws of civilization.
- Serve on behalf of your Crown and Countrymen.
- Heed the call, no matter the danger.

### Level 3: Champion's Challenge

As a Bonus Action, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity to issue a challenge that compels other creatures to do battle with you. Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature can't willingly move more than 30 feet away from you. This effect ends on the creature if you are [[Incapacitated]] or die or if the creature is more than 30 feet away from you.

### Level 3: Turn the Tide

As a Bonus Action, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity to bolster injured creatures. Each creature of your choice that can hear you within a 30-foot [[Emanation]] regains [[Hit Points]] equal to two times your Paladin level + your spellcasting ability modifier if it has no more than half of its hit points. Once a creature receives this benefit, that creature can't receive it again until after they finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

### Level 7: Devout Allegiance

When a creature within your Aura of Protection takes damage, you can use your Reaction to magically substitute your own health for that of the target creature, causing that creature not to take the damage. Instead, you take the damage. This damage to you can't be reduced or prevented in any way.

### Level 15: Unyielding Apostle

When you start your turn with the [[Incapacitated]] condition you can immediately end the [[Paralyzed]] or [[Stunned]] condition on yourself and regain a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to your Paladin level.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].

### Level 20: Exalted Champion

As a Bonus Action, you can cause your presence on the field of battle to be an inspiration to those dedicated to your cause, granting the benefits below for 10 minutes or until you end them (no action required). Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest]]. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (no action required).

**Unconquerable.** You have [[Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

**Crown's Ward.** Your allies have [[Advantage]] on [[Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]] while within your Aura of Protection. Additionally, you and your allies within your Aura of Protection have [[Advantage]] on Wisdom saving throws.
