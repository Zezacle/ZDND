### a.k.a. Jotun Tongue

The modern language of the Giants' descendants, Jotun Tongue is said to descend from the first beings themselves. The original script is said to be made of the same symbols that fueled the Greater Giant's powerful magic, however it was lost to time. The spoken word of the Giants persisted through the Lesser Giants, Trolls, [[Firbolgs]] and [[Goliaths]].

Eventually, trade with [[Midgard]] necessitated a script, and so they adopted the Dvergarian script for their language. The lost script of the Greater Giants is known as ‘True Giant,' however both use the same speech known simply as Giant or ‘Jotun Tongue'.

**Script:** Dvergarian Runic Alphabet
**Writing System:** Giant (left-to-right)

**Real World Analog:** Ukrainian
