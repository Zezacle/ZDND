The language of Dragons, Draconic was birthed by the Great Dreki to fit their tongues and subsequently passed to their kin. Draconic is a slow-spoken language that didn't have script until after Ragnarök when the Dragonborn adapted the Dvergarian runes into their own Draconic writing.

**Medium:** Dvergarian Runic Alphabet
**Script:** Draconic (top-to-bottom, left-to-right)

**Real World Analog:** Mongolian
