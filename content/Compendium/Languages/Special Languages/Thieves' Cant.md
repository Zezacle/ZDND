As a spoken "language" [[Thieves' Cant]] is really an inconspicuous code woven into regular speech. As a written code, it consists of markings that can denote danger, treasure, hiding spots and many other things.

It is used by thieves and criminals to mark areas and warn others about certain dangers or useful opportunities. For example a town house might have a marking denoting that the owner doesn't like the local authorities and will shelter outlaws.

Leaving these markings is a time-honored tradition for those who skirt the law.
