With the melding of cultures in [[Midgard]], the old Languages mixed and were eventually formed into a new language that borrowed from many sources. The Common tongue was born, using a much simplified version of the Dvergarian Runes as the alphabet and using mostly [[Celestial]] words and sounds.

In the current age, it is rare to find a Sapient who isn't at least partially fluent in the Common tongue.

**Script:** Common Alphabet
**Writing System:** Common (left-to-right)

**Real World Analog:** English
