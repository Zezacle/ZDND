There are many different species of Sapient people within the Collapsed Realms. Each species has varying Races, or groups, divided by some difference in anatomy.

You chosen Race grants an **Ability Score Increase** or Stat buff as well as a number of special Racial Features, from seeing in the dark to extra movement speed.

### Species Rarities

The table below explains the rarities of races in relation to common society.

| Rarity    | Description                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Common    | These races are everywhere. It is not unusual for a person to interact with multitudes of them in the course of their life.                                       |
| Uncommon  | These races are scarce, but well known enough that seeing one won't necessarily raise eyebrows.                                                                   |
| Rare      | These races are known to common folk, but a commoner is unlikely to see one in the course of their life unless they were to seek them out.                        |
| Very Rare | These races are treated more-so as myths than real to common folk. A member of a very rare race walking into a town might cause a commotion.                      |
| Unique    | These races are unheard of or otherwise treated as mythical by common folk. They are more likely to be confused as a mutant of another race than their own thing. |


### Species Descriptions

#### Beastfolk

Beastfolk are a large collection of various races originally native to the wilds of Vanaheim. It is theorized that they were originally simple animals morphed by the latent Vanir magic scattered within the wilds. The variety of Beastfolk is wide, including [[Lizardfolk]], [[Catfolk]], [[Birdfolk]], [[Harefolk]], [[Turtlefolk]], [[Rodentians]], and more.

#### Draconic
Draconic describes Dragons or races with lineage to the Great Dreki, or the great powerful dragons of old. They are [[Dragonborn]] and [[Kobolds]].

#### Dvergar
Dvergar are the hardy species once native to Svartalfheim. There are three races of Dvergar: [[Dwarves]], [[Gnomes]] and [[Orcs]].

#### Elves
Elves are the primary sapient species of Alfheim. They are a long-lived species that can be found uncommonly in every realm. They feature tall, lithe bodies with long pointed ears. There are [[Fae]] or 'High' Elves, [[Grae]] or 'Low' Elves and [[Mortae]] the 'Middle' or Wood Elves.

#### Goblinoids
Goblinoids are a group of races once native to the Feywild of Alfheim, however somehow they colonized large areas of Midgard's forests and caves before the collapse of the realms. Despite highly varying appearances, [[Goblins]], [[Hobgoblins]] and [[Bugbears]] all share a heritage.

#### Jotuns
Jotuns, also known simply as Giants, are the species once native to Jotunheim. The first beings were the Celestial Giants that would birth the Aesir Gods. In the current era, there are the common lesser Giants such as [[Goliaths]] and [[Firbolgs]], as well the feral Trolls. There are legends that some of the Greater Giants survived Thor's massacre and the collapse of the realms.

#### Men
The Men of Midgard are a hearty and adaptable race of mortals, known for their expansive societies. There are [[Humans]] and [[Halflings]]. Additionally, Men are highly susceptible to the influence of both Gods and Fiends resulting in the birth of [[Godkin]] and [[Fiendlings]], respectively.
