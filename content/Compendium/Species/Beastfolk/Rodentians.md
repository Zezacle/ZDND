The Rodentians are the group of Beastfolk resembling Rats, Mice, and Squirrels. They are a prideful clan of surprising bravery.

## Rodentian Features

**Rarity:** Rare
**Native Language:** [[Beast Speech]]

**Age:** 50 years, Mature at 10
**Size:** Small
- **Height:** 3ft - 4ft *(90cm - 120cm)*
- **Weight:** 35lbs - 50lbs *(16kg - 22kg)*
- **Speed:** 30ft *(6 sq.)*, [[Crawling]] 30ft *(6 sq.)*

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

**Rodent's Nimbleness.** You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than you, but you can't stop in the same space.

**Scamper.** You have a [[Crawling]] speed of 30 feet and standing up from [[Prone]] only costs 5 feet of movement. Additionally, while [[Crawling]], any [[Opportunity Attacks]] made against you are have [[Disadvantage]]. 

**Naturally Stealthy.** You can take the [[Hide]] action even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.

**Rodent Lineage.** You share ancestry with a kind of rodent. Choose one of the following to determine your appearance, Ability Score Increase and bonus racial feat.

**Ratfolk.** You are a small bipedal Rat.
- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.
- **Darkvision.** You can see in [[Dim Light]] within 30 feet of you as if it were [[Bright Light]], and in [[Darkness]] as if it were [[Dim Light]]. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

**Mousefolk.** You are a small bipedal Mouse.
- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.
- **Keen Senses.** You have proficiency in the [[Perception]] skill. 

**Squirrelfolk.** You are a small bipedal Squirrel.
- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.
- **Climb.** You have a [[Climbing]] speed of 30 feet.
