Raccoonfolk are a race of Beastfolk resembling raccoons. Their ancestor's curiosity and cunning lives on within them.

## Raccoonfolk Features

**Rarity:** Rare
**Known Languages:** [[Common]] and [[Beast Tongue]]

**Age:** 80 years, Mature at 16
**Size:** Small
- **Height:** 3.5ft - 4.5ft *(100cm - 140cm)*
- **Weight:** 65lbs - 120lbs *(30kg - 55kg)*
- **Speed:** 25 ft. *(5 sq.)*, [[Climbing|Climb]] 25 ft., [[Crawling|Crawl]] 25 ft.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1. 

**Darkvision.** You have [[Darkvision]] with a range of 30 feet.

**Hardened Stomach.** You have [[Resistance]] to damage inflicted by an ingested substance and [[Advantage]] on saves against effects caused by ingesting a substance.

**Ingenuity.** When you, or another creature you are [[Help|Helping]], fail an ability check that uses Wisdom or Intelligence, you can roll a d4 and add it to the result, potentially turning the failure into a success. 

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Masked Sleuth.** If you are not already proficient in either the [[Sleight of Hand]] and [[Investigation]] skills, you have [[Half Proficiency]] in those skills.

**Quadruped Adept.** You have a [[Climbing]] and [[Crawling]] speed of 25 feet.
