Half-Elves are a blend of human and elf, sharing in their boons and limitations.

## Half-Elf Features

**Rarity:** Uncommon
**Native Language:** [[Elvish]]

**Age:** 150 years, Mature at 20
**Size:** Medium
- **Height:** 5ft - 6.5ft *(150cm - 200cm)*
- **Weight:** 100lbs - 300lbs *(45kg - 135kg)*
- **Speed:** 30 ft. *(6 sq.)*

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.

**Elven Ancestry.** You have a boon from your elven heritage. Choose one of the following options:
- **Fae Elf.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws against being [[Charmed]]. You can complete a [[Long Rest]] in 6 hours, only 4 hours of which need to be sleep. Additionally, magic can’t put you to sleep.
- **Grae Elf.** You have [[Darkvision]] with a range of 30 feet.
- **Mortae Elf.** Your [[Speed]] increases by 5 feet.

**Skillful.** You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice.

**Versatile.** You gain an [[Origin Feats|Origin Feat]] of your choice.
