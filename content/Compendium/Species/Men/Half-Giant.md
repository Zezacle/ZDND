Half-Giants are a blend of human and giant blood, sharing in their boons and limitations.

## Half-Giant Features

**Rarity:** Rare
**Native Language:** [[Giant]]

**Age:** 100 years, Mature at 20
**Size:** Medium
- **Height:** 6.5 ft. - 7.5 ft. *(200 cm - 215 cm)*
- **Weight:** 180 lb. - 300 lb. *(80 kg - 135 kg)*
- **Speed:** 30 ft. *(6 sq.)*

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three different scores by 1. You can't raise any of your scores above 20.

**Powerful Build.** You have [[Advantage]] on any ability check you make to end the [[Grappled]] condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

**Stone's Endurance.** You can focus yourself to occasionally shrug off injury. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a d8. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled, and reduce the damage by that total. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest]].

**Skillful.** You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice.
