While you have the Bleeding condition, you experience the following effects.

***Bleeding Levels.*** This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Bleeding level.

***Bloodletting.*** At the start of each of your turns, you take 1d4 Necrotic damage per level of Bleeding.

***Removing Bleeding Levels.*** Any kind of Healing removes all levels of Bleeding. Alternatively, as an Action, you can make a DC 15 Wisdom ([[Medicine]]) check, removing two levels of Bleeding on a success.

*Notes:*
- This condition represents significant and ongoing blood loss, it cannot be inflicted without explicit gameplay mechanics or extenuating circumstances.
