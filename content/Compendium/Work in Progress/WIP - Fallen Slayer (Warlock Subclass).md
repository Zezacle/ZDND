This is a yet-to-be playtested, WIP Warlock subclass concept by Grim Angel, reworked for D&D 5.5e (2024) by Zee.

*You are bound to a ghost of genocide, a warrior whose name was once screamed in terror by the Divine. They carved a path through celestial and demonic realms alike until the sheer weight of the abyss dragged them down. Now, they are a restless echo inhabiting your armaments. They don't want your soul—they want your mind and body, to finish the war they started. When you draw your armament, it is a conduit for a thousand years of combat muscle memory.*

### Level 3: Fallen Slayer Spells
The magic of your patron ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Warlock level specified in the Fallen Slayer Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 3: Slayer's Body
If you are not already proficient in either the [[Athletics]] and [[Acrobatics]] skills, you have [[Half Proficiency]] in those skills.

### Level 3: Vessel of the Slayer
Your body has been tempered to withstand the rigors of your patron's eternal crusade. You gain training with [[Medium Armor]] and [[Shields]], as well as proficiency with Martial Weapons.

Additionally, you can use any weapon you are proficient with as a Spellcasting Focus for your Warlock spells.

### Level 6: Slayer's Spirit
You can channel the Slayer's Spirit to fuel magical effects. Two effects are available to you: *Slayer's Guidance* and *Crimson Flow*. You can channel the Slayer's Spirit a number of times equal to your Charisma Bonus (minimum once) per Short Rest.

#### Slayer's Guidance
When you make a weapon attack roll, you can add 1d6 to the roll.

Your Slayer's Guidance die changes when you reach certain Warlock levels. The die becomes a d8 at level 10, and a d10 at level 14.

#### Crimson Flow
When you reduce a creature to 0 [[Hit Points]] with a weapon attack, you can use your Reaction to move up to half your [[Speed]] and make an additional weapon attack against a different target. This movement does not provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]].

### Level 10: Slayer’s Mind
You gain [[Resistance]] to Psychic damage and [[Immunity]] to the [[Frightened]] condition.

### Level 10: Slayer's Defiance
If you are reduced to 0 [[Hit Points]] but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Each creature of your choice in a 20-foot [[Emanation]] originating from you makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 3d8 Psychic damage and is [[Stunned]] until they the next time they take damage or the start of your next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

Once you use this feature, you gain a level of [[Exhaustion]] and cannot use this feature again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

### Level 14: Possession of the Slayer
 As a [[Magic]] action, you allow the spirit of the Slayer to fully possess your form. Each creature of your choice in a 20-foot [[Emanation]] originating from you makes a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 3d8 Force damage and is knocked [[Prone]]. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

The possession lasts for up to 1 minute and ends early if you become [[Unconscious]]. While possessed, you gain the following features:

#### Extra Attack
You can make one additional attack whenever you take the [[Attack]] action on your turn.

#### Improved Slayer Channeling
You regain all expended uses of Slayer's Spirit, and the available effects are amplified:
- **Slayer's Precision.** When you make a weapon attack roll, you can add 1d12 to the roll. If the attack hits, you deal additional damage equal to the result of the d12's roll.
- **Crimson Flood.** When you reduce a creature to 0 [[Hit Points]], you can teleport up to 30 feet and make one weapon attack against another target. If that attack also reduces a creature to 0 [[Hit Points]], you can teleport an additional 30 feet and make one weapon attack against another target.

#### Possessed Soul
You have [[Immunity]] to the [[Charmed]] condition.


Once this feature's effects end, you gain a level of [[Exhaustion]] and you cannot use this feature again until you finish a [[Long Rest]].

Starting at 18th level, you no longer suffer [[Exhaustion]] from using this feature.



### Changes made by Zee:
- Reformatted and rewritten to fit 2024 rules and nomenclature
- Removed language limiting abilities to Melee, allowing Ranged Weapon playstyles
- Lots of Nerfs, kinda was too much compared to other 2024 subclasses

Specific Notes:
- Reduced extra spells because this is such a Martial heavy subclass.
- Moved Acrobatics and Athletics to Lv3, nerfed to half-proficiency
- Removed Fighting Style: can still be gotten through feats, didn't really want subclass outshining Martial classes
- Slayer's Guidance and Crimson Flow: Merged into one resource pool that refreshes on Short Rest, built kinda like a Cleric/Paladin Channel Divinity
	- Slayer's Guidance: removed extra damage due to it now being refreshed on Short Rest
	- Crimson Flow now allows Ranged weapon attacks.
- Separated Slayer's Defiance's resistances and immunity into Slayer's Mind and changed damage type from Necrotic to Psychic (Mind, Body, Spirit motif)
- Slayer's Defiance: changed Necro dmg + prone --> Psychic damage + Stunned; stun means its now more "team come save me please".
- Possession of the Slayer: Reworked substantially
	- Improved Slayer Channeling: Restores uses of and Buffs both Slayer's Guidance and Crimson Flow, requires expending uses still to avoid a full minute of Supermanning around.
	- added Charmed immunity, for that "unbendable will of a god" feeling and prevent it from being used against your party
	- Added exhaustion level when possession ends, no exhaustion when Lv18+
