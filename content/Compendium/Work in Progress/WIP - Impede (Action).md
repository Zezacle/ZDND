When you take the [[WIP - Impede (Action)]] action, you create a guarded area centered on you with a radius equal to half of your remaining movement speed (minimum 5-foot-radius aka neighboring spaces).

When a creature enters or starts moving through your guarded area you can speed movement to move intercept them, making a single melee [[Attack]] against them and using the *Impede* feature below.

**Impede.** You make a contested [[Athletics]] check against the target's [[Athletics]] or [[Acrobatics]]. If you succeed the contest, the creature's [[Speed]] is reduced to 0. If the target has taken the [[Disengage]] action, then they have [[Advantage]] on their check.

Your guarded area lasts until the start of your next turn as long as you have movement speed.
