*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Fighting Style.** Choose a Fighting Style Feat. 

**Martial Adaptiveness.** When you reach character levels 8, 12, 16 and 20 you can change your Fighting Style Feat to a different one of your choice.
