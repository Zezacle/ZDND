*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Piercing Master.** Once per turn, when you deal Piercing damage, you can apply either the [[Sap]] or [[Vex]] Mastery property. You cannot apply multiple Mastery properties to the same attack. 

**Enhanced Critical.** When you score a [[Critical Hit]] that deals Piercing damage to a creature, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the Piercing damage the target takes.
