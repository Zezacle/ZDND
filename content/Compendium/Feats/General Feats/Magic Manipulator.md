*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Charisma 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Mind Freak.** When you deal psychic damage to a target, you become [[Invisible]] to the target until the end of your current turn.

**Slander.** You learn the [[Vicious Mockery]] cantrip. If you already know [[Vicious Mockery]] you can add your Charisma modifier to the damage rolls.

**"Who? Me?"** If a creature you've [[Charmed]] would know it was [[Charmed]] when effect ends, it doesn't.
