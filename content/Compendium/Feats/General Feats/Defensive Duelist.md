*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Parry.** If you're holding a [[Defensive]] or [[Finesse]] weapon and another creature hits you with a melee attack, you can take a Reaction to add a bonus determined by your weapon's properties. If the weapon has both properties, you gain the bonus for both:
- [[Defensive]]. Add your Proficiency Bonus to your [[Armor Class]], potentially causing the attack to deal reduced damage.
- [[Finesse]]. Add your Proficiency Bonus to your [[Evasion Class]], potentially causing the attack to miss you.

You gain this bonus until the start of your next turn unless you become [[Incapacitated]] or are [[Disarm|Disarmed]].

**Riposte.** If you cause an attack's damage to be reduced or negated using a *Parry*, you can make a single melee attack against the creature that attacked you. You can only use this feature once per round.
