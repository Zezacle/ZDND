*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Armored.** While you’re wearing [[Light Armor]], [[Medium Armor]], or [[Heavy Armor]], you gain a +1 bonus to [[Armor Class]].

**Interception.** When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage dealt to the target by 1d8 plus your Proficiency Bonus. You must be holding a [[Shields|Shield]] or a melee [[Weapons|Weapon]] to use this Reaction.
