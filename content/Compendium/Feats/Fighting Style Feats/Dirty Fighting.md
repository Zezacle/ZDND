*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Dirty Fighting.** Once per turn when you hit a target with a weapon attack you can choose to deal half damage and impose one of the following effects onto the target until it takes damage or the end of its next turn:
* [[Disadvantage]] on target's next Attack Roll
* [[Disadvantage]] on target's next Saving Throw
* Reduce target's speed by 10 feet
