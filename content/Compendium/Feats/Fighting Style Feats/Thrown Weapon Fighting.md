*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

When you hit with a ranged attack using a [[Thrown]] weapon, you can add your Strength and Dexterity to the damage roll.

When using the [[Quick Toss]] bonus action: 
- If you are throwing a weapon with the [[Thrown]] property it is not considered an [[Improvised Weapons|Improvised Weapon]] for the attack.
- If you are throwing anything else, you do not have [[Disadvantage]] from the [[Quick Toss]] if your target is 10 feet away or less.
