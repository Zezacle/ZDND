*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Powerful Throw.** When you hit with a ranged attack using a [[Thrown]] weapon, you can add your Strength and Dexterity to the damage roll.

**Chucker.** When using the [[Quick Toss]] bonus action: 
- If you are throwing a weapon with the [[Thrown]] property it is not considered an [[Improvised Weapons|Improvised Weapon]] for the attack and therefore can be thrown to its max range.
- If you are throwing anything else, you do not have [[Disadvantage]] from the [[Quick Toss]] if your target is 10 feet away or less.

**Yah-YEET!** You can make ranged attacks with [[Thrown]] weapons up to double their long range at [[Disadvantage]]. This attack only hits on a [[Critical Hit]], but multiplies the Critical Hit damage by four.

*This is a 1 in 400 (0.25%) chance of hitting without buffs like Champion Fighter's expanded critical range.*
