*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Point Blank.** When making a ranged attack while you are within 5 feet of a hostile creature, you do not have [[Disadvantage]] on the attack roll. 

**Short-Range Precision.** Your ranged attacks ignore [[Half Cover]] and [[Three-Quarters Cover]] against targets within 30 feet of you. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls on ranged attacks.
