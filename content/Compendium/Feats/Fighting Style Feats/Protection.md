*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Guardian.** When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can take a Reaction to interpose your [[Shields|Shield]] if you're holding one. You impose [[Disadvantage]] on the triggering attack roll and all other attack rolls against the target until the start of your next turn if you remain within 5 feet of the target.

**Savior.** Your speed is increased by 10 feet if you are moving in the direction of an [[Incapacitated]] ally. You have [[Advantage]] on [[Medicine]] checks to stabilize an [[Incapacitated]] creature.

**Defender.** While within 5 feet of an [[Incapacitated]] creature, you gain a +2 to attack rolls made against targets hostile to the [[Incapacitated]] creature.
