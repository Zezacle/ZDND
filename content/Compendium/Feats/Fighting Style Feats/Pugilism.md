*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

**Pugilist Weapons.** You gain proficiency with [[Knuckles]] and [[Punching Gauntlet|Punching Gauntlets]].

**Float Like a Butterfly.** If both of your hands are empty or holding [[Knuckles]] or [[Punching Gauntlet|Punching Gauntlets]] you gain a +1 bonus to your [[Evasion Class]].

**Sting Like a Bee.** Damage Rolls you make with [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]], [[Knuckles]] and [[Punching Gauntlet|Punching Gauntlets]] can benefit from both your Strength and Dexterity modifiers.
