*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

Your speed is increased by 10 feet if you are moving in the direction of an [[Incapacitated]] ally. You have [[Advantage]] on [[Medicine]] checks to stabilize an [[Incapacitated]] creature.

Additionally, while within 5 feet of an [[Incapacitated]] creature, you gain a +1 to attack rolls made against targets hostile to the [[Incapacitated]] creature.
