Magic is categorized into nine primary schools of magic.

| School        | Description                                                                                                 | Example Spell                       |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Abjuration    | Spells of protection, negation, and banishment.                                                             | [[Arcane Shield]], [[Counterspell]] |
| Conjuration   | Spells that involve summoning creatures, transporting objects, or teleportation.                            | [[Find Familiar]], [[Misty Step]]   |
| Divination    | Spells that reveal information, locate objects, or see the future.                                          | [[Detect Magic]], [[Identify]]      |
| Enchantment   | Spells that manipulate, influence, or charm the minds of others.                                            | [[Charm Person]], [[Hold Monster]]  |
| Evocation     | Spells that manipulate magical energy to produce powerful effects, such as raw elemental damage or healing. | [[Fireball]], [[Cure Wounds]]       |
| Illusion      | Spells that deceive the senses or the mind, creating phantasms or false appearances.                        | [[Minor Illusion]], [[Blur]]        |
| Necromancy    | Spells that manipulate the energy of life, death, and undeath.                                              | [[Animate Dead]], [[Revivify]]      |
| Transmutation | Spells that modify energy or matter, changing the physical properties of objects or creatures.              | [[Mage Hand]], [[Polymorph]]        |
#### Specialized Magic
Some powerful, specialized magic of certain schools are known under a different name.

| Sub-School  | School        | Description                                                        |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Biomancy    | Transmutation | The magical alteration of flesh, flora, and biological forms.      |
| Chronomancy | Transmutation | The study and manipulation of time.                                |
| Dunamancy   | Transmutation | Manipulation of space, dimensions and gravitational energy.        |
| Hemomancy   | Necromancy    | Magic that relies on the life force and blood of living creatures. |
| Oneiromancy | Divination    | The manipulation and interpretation of dreams and prophecies.      |
