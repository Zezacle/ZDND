When you take the [[Brace]] Reaction, you have the following effects until the end of your next turn: 
- You cannot take an Action on your next turn.
- Your [[Speed]] is halved.
- Your [[Evasion Class]] is reduced by 5, but your [[Armor Class]] increases by your proficiency bonus.
- You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws to maintain [[Concentration]].
- If forced to make a Dexterity saving throw, it is instead a Strength or Constitution saving throw (DM's choice.)
- You cannot be forced [[Prone]] unless by falling.


You lose these benefits if you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition.
