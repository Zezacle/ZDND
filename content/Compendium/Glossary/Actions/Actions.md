On your turn, you are able to take one Action and a Bonus Action as well as use your movement. Certain acts are considered "Free Actions" such as picking up a weapon from the ground, or saying a quip or simple instruction in combat.

Additionally, during the time between the end of your turn and start of your next turn, you have one Reaction, which is something you can do in response to specific triggers.


### Actions
On your turn in combat, you can take one action. Choose which action to take from those below or from the special actions provided by your features.

- [[Attack]] - Hurt a target
- [[Brace]] - Reduce incoming damage
- [[Dash]] - Gain extra movement
- [[Disengage]] - Avoid Opportunity Attacks
- [[Dodge]] - Reduce likelihood of getting hit
- [[Help]] - Aid an ally's efforts
- [[Hide]] - Attempt to obscure yourself
- [[Influence]] - Attempt to persuade, intimidate or deceive a target
- [[Magic]] - Cast Spells and use other forms of Magic
- [[Ready]] - Delay a different action until a triggering circumstance is met
- [[Search]] - Use your senses to look for something
- [[Study]] - Attempt to understand or recall something
- [[Utilize]] - Interact with something

### Bonus Actions
On your turn in combat, you can take one Bonus Action. Usually, this is for special class features or spells, but there are a few standard Bonus Actions available to everyone.

- [[Dash]] (BA) - Gain a little extra movement
- [[Disengage]] (BA) - Impair incoming Opportunity Attacks
- [[Quick Toss]] - Chuck something recklessly

### Reactions
In combat, during the time between the end of your turn and start of your next turn, you have one Reaction, which is something you can do in response to specific triggers.

- [[Opportunity Attacks]] - Melee strike when a target tries to run away
- [[Ready]] Action's Held Action - Trigger your held action
