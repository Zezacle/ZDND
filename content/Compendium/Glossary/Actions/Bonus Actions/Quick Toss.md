When you take the [[Quick Toss]] bonus action, you throw whatever is in your hands and make a ranged attack roll at [[Disadvantage]] against a target within 30 feet. Whatever you throw is considered an [[Improvised Weapons|Improvised Weapon]].

Additionally, you can Quick Toss an item or object to an ally. They must use their Reaction to catch it, otherwise it lands at their feet. The item may break on impact if fragile.
