When you take the [[Disarm]] action, make a melee attack roll with a weapon or [[Unarmed Strike]] contested by the target's Strength or Dexterity saving throw. On a fail, the target drops one object of your choice that it's holding, with the object landing in its space.

The target has [[Advantage]] on their saving throw if they are holding what you are trying disarm them of with at least two hands.

If you have a target [[Grappled]], you have [[Advantage]] on your attack roll to disarm them.
