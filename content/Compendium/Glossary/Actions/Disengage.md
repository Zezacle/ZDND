This action can be used as an Action or Bonus Action changing it's effectiveness. If an ability says "You can use the [[Disengage]] action as a Bonus Action", always use the "As an Action" variant.

#### As an Action
When you take the [[Disengage]] action, your movement does not provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]] from until the end of your turn.

#### As a Bonus Action
When you take the [[Disengage]] Bonus Action, [[Opportunity Attacks]] made against you have [[Disadvantage]] until the end of your turn.
