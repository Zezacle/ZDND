When you take the Sprint action, you immediately move a distance of up to three times you [[Speed]], after applying any modifiers. You can't take this action again until the start of your next turn (no [[Haste]] double sprint).

If you have a special Speed, such as a [[Flying]] Speed or [[Swimming]] Speed, you can use that speed instead when you take this action. You choose which speed to use each time you Sprint.

At the end of every turn on which you take the Sprint action, you must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, you gain a level of temporary [[Exhaustion]].

A creature can catch its breath by taking no action for 10 consecutive minutes, removing all [[Exhaustion]] gained from Sprinting.

### Example
With a [[Speed]] of 30 feet, the Sprint action would let you move 90 feet, meaning you can move up to 120 feet on your turn. If your Speed of 30 feet is reduced to 15 feet, that distance goes down to 60 feet.
