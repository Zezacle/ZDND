Damage is categorized into varying types. The type associated with a source of damage may influence the amount of damage inflicted based on the target's damage [[Resistance|Resistances]], [[Vulnerability|Vulnerabilities]], or [[Immunity|Immunities]].

| Type        | Description                          | Example                            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Acid        | Chemical burns and reactions         | [[Acid Vial]]                      |
| Bludgeoning | Blunt impacts and falling            | [[Warhammer]], Slam attacks        |
| Cold        | Frost and water                      | [[Ray of Frost]]                   |
| Fire        | Heat                                 | [[Fireball]], [[Torch]]            |
| Force       | Pure magical energy                  | [[Magic Missile]]                  |
| Lightning   | Electricity                          | [[Lightning Bolt]]                 |
| Necrotic    | Decay and life force affecting magic | [[Inflict Wounds]]                 |
| Piercing    | Penetrating impacts                  | [[Spear]], Bite attacks            |
| Poison      | Reaction to toxic substances         | [[Poison\|Poisons]], Venom attacks |
| Psychic     | Harmful mental energy                | [[Mind Spike]]                     |
| Radiant     | Divine or pure energy                | [[Divine Smite]]                   |
| Slashing    | Cuts                                 | [[Longsword]], Claw attacks        |
| Thunder     | Sonic or concussive force            | [[Thunderwave]], Explosions        |
