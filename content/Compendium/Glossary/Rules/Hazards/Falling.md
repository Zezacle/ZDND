A creature that falls takes 1d6 Bludgeoning damage at the end of the fall for every 10 feet it fell. When the creature lands, it has the [[Prone]] condition unless it avoids taking any damage from the fall.

If the creature jumps willingly, it can fall up to 20 feet (or 10 feet for creatures wearing [[Hefty]] armor) before [[Falling|Fall Damage]] starts incrementing.

A creature that falls into water or another liquid can use its Reaction to make a DC 15 Strength ([[Athletics]]) or Dexterity ([[Acrobatics]]) check to hit the surface head or feet first. On a successful check, any damage resulting from the fall is halved.
