A creature is [[Stable]] if it has 0 [[Hit Points]] but isn't required to make [[Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]]. 

A stabilized creature that is not [[Unconscious]] can make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw on each of it's turns. Gaining 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] on a success, ending their turn.
