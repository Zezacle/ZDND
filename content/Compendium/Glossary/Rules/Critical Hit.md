If you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll, you score a [[Critical Hit]], and the attack hits regardless of any modifiers or the target's [[Evasion Class|EC]] or [[Armor Class|AC]].

Critical Hits deal the maximum roll of the attack's damage dice, plus one of the following effects to the target:

| Damage Type Dealt | Effect                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Any               | Extra Damage: Roll normal damage in addition to Crit Damage                                                 |
|                   |                                                                                                             |
| Acid              | Permanently reduce [[Armor Class\|AC]] by 1                                                                 |
| Bludgeoning       | Inflict [[Dazed]] until start of your next turn                                                             |
| Cold              | Permanently reduce [[Evasion Class\|EC]] by 2                                                               |
| Fire              | Inflict [[Burning]]                                                                                         |
| Force             | -                                                                                                           |
| Lightning         | Permanently reduce [[Evasion Class\|EC]] by 2                                                               |
| Necrotic          | Inflict a level of [[Bleeding]]                                                                             |
| Piercing          | Permanently reduce [[Armor Class\|AC]] by 1                                                                 |
| Poison            | Inflict [[Poisoned]]: DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw at the end of each of target's turns to end condition |
| Psychic           | Inflict [[Blinded]] until start of your next turn                                                           |
| Radiant           | Inflict [[Blinded]] until start of your next turn                                                           |
| Slashing          | Inflict a level of [[Bleeding]]                                                                             |
| Thunder           | Inflict [[Dazed]] until start of your next turn                                                             |
