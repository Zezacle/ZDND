If you roll a 20 on the d20 for an attack roll, you score a [[Critical Hit]], and the attack hits regardless of any modifiers or the target's [[Armor Class|AC]].

Critical Hits deal the maximum roll of the attack's damage dice, plus your normal damage bonus, plus a normal roll of the damage dice.

For example, a weapon that deals 2d6 + 3 damage would deal:
- 12 (dice max.) 
+ + 3 (damage bonus) 
+ + 2d6 (normal damage dice)

Or a spell that deals 4d6 damage would deal:
- 24 (dice max.)
- + 4d6 (normal damage dice)
