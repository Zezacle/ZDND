For some magic items that recharge uses, they do so at a [[Holy Dawn]]. The definition of a [[Holy Dawn]] is different based on the resting ruleset being used for the campaign.

#### Standard Resting Rules
A [[Holy Dawn]] happens at the sunrise of every day.

#### [[Excursion Resting]] Rules
A [[Holy Dawn]] happens at the sunrise of the seventh day of the week.
