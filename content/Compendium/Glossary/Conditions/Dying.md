While you have 0 [[Hit Points]], you have the [[Dying]] condition until you regain [[Hit Points]], become [[Stable]], or are [[Dead]]. While Dying you experience the following effects.


***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have [[Advantage]].

***Brink of Death.*** If you fail a [[Death Saving Throw]], you gain the [[Unconscious]] condition.

***Death Crawl.*** You fall [[Prone]]. If still conscious, you may move up to 5 feet on your turn. When this condition ends, you remain [[Prone]].

***Exhaustion.*** You gain 1 level of [[Exhaustion]].

***Faltering Speech.*** You cannot speak clearly or loudly.

***Inactive.*** You can't take any Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction on your turn other than rolling a [[Death Saving Throw]].

***No Concentration.*** Your [[Concentration]] is broken.

***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.

****

***Damage at 0 Hit Points.** If you take any damage while you have 0 [[Hit Points]], you suffer a [[Death Saving Throw]] failure. If the damage is from a [[Critical Hit]], you suffer two failures instead. If the damage equals or exceeds your [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum, you die.*
