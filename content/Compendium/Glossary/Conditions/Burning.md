While you have the Burning condition, you experience the following effects.

***Burn Damage.*** A burning creature or object takes 1d6 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. 

***Removing Burning Condition.*** As an Action, you can extinguish fire on yourself by giving yourself the [[Prone]] condition and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated.
