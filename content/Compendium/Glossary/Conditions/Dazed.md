While you have the [[Dazed]] condition, you experience the following effects.

***Deafened.*** You have the [[Deafened]] condition.

***Saving Throws Affected.*** You have [[Disadvantage]] on saving throws.

***Slowed.*** Your [[Speed]] is halved.

***Attacks Affected.*** You have [[Disadvantage]] on attack rolls. Attack rolls against you have [[Advantage]].
