[[Silvered]] describes weapons or ammunition made of or inlayed with silver. When you hit a target with a shape shifting ability, such as a Vampire, with a silvered weapon or ammunition, it deals Force damage instead of its regular damage type.

These strikes can still benefit from effects relying on the weapon's native damage type even when dealing Force damage. For example, a Silvered [[Light Hammer]] can benefit from the [[Crusher]] feat's [[Push]] on bludgeoning hits effect on Force damage hits.

### Silvered Weapons
Silvered weapons cannot benefit from other [[Masterwork]] properties such as [[+1 Weapon]].

Silvered weapons costs the **normal weapon's cost times 5 plus 100 GP.**

For example, a Silvered [[Longsword]] (normally 15 GP) costs 175 GP:
- (15 GP x 5) + 100 GP = 45 GP

### Silvered Ammunition
When attempting to recover expended Silvered Ammunition, you often find the silvered tips blunted or destroyed on impact. You can only recover a quarter of your expended ammunition.

Silvered ammunition costs the **normal ammunition's cost times 5 plus 75 GP.**

For example, a bundle of Silvered [[Arrows]] (normally 1 GP) costs 80 GP:
- (1 GP x 5) + 75 GP = 80 GP
