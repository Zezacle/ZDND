[[+1 Weapon]] describes Masterwork weapons that have been given a perfect edge or balance.

You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with a [[+1 Weapon]].

### +1 Weapon Cost
The cost of a +1 Weapon is the **normal weapon's cost times 10 plus 100 GP.**

For example a +1 [[Longsword]] (15 GP) would cost 250 GP:
- (15 GP x 10) + 100 GP = 250 GP
