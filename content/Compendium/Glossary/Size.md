The [[Size]] of objects and creatures is categorized in one of six categories.

| Size       | Space                    | Example            |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Tiny       | 2.5 x 2.5 ft             | Rat, Spider        |
| Small      | 5 x 5 ft.                | Goblins, Halflings |
| Medium     | 5 x 5 ft.                | Most Humanoids     |
| Large      | 10 x 10 ft.              | Ogre               |
| Huge       | 15 x 15 ft.              | Giants             |
| Gargantuan | 20 x 20 ft.<br>or larger | Kraken             |
