_Abjuration Cantrip (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Whenever a creature makes a weapon attack roll against you before the spell ends, the attacker subtracts 1d4 from the attack roll.
