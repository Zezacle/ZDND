*Abjuration Cantrip (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch an ally creature and channel their inner life force to cause a surge of natural healing. If the target creature has at least 1 hit point, it can spend one of its [[Hit Point Dice]], rolling the die and adding their Constitution modifier to the result. They regain [[Hit Points]] equal to the total (minimum of 0).

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** A creature touched by this spell can spend one additional Hit Die when you reach 5th level (2), 11th level (3), and 17th level (4).
