_Level 5 Transmutation (Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S, M (a Quiver worth 1+ GP)  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

When you cast the spell and as a Bonus Action until it ends, you can make two attacks with a weapon that fires [[Arrows]], [[Heavy Arrows]], [[Bolts]], or [[Compact Bolts]] such as a [[Longbow]] or a [[Light Crossbow]]. The spell magically creates the ammunition needed for each attack. Each arrow or bolt created by the spell deals damage like a nonmagical piece of ammunition of its kind and disintegrates immediately after it hits or misses.
