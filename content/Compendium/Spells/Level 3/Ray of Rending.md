*Level 3 Necromancy (Cleric, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You shoot three reddish-black rays at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Necrotic damage and gains a level of the [[Bleeding]] condition.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You create one additional ray for each spell slot level above 3.
