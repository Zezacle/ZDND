*Level 3 Enchantment (Cleric, Paladin)*

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Imbue your bludgeoning and force strikes to put a creature to sleep, complete with a pillow to cushion their head when they hit the ground. When you hit a creature with bludgeoning or force damage, the target must make a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Unconscious]] condition for 1 minute.

Each time you consecutively hit a target while the spell is active, your spell save DC, for that target on this spell specifically, increases by 1. This bonus resets if a target goes 1 round without making a save against this spell.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The time the target sleep increases by 1 minute for each spell slot level above 3.
