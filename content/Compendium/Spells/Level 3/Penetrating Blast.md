*Level 3 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock)*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a metal drill bit)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a point on a tangible barrier that you can see within range that is no thicker than 15 feet. On the other side of the barrier, you create an explosive shockwave. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Cone]] originating on the other side of the targeted point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d10 Thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

The spell fails and the spell slot is not consumed if the targeted barrier is thicker than 15 feet or has any amount of lead in the spell's path.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 3.
