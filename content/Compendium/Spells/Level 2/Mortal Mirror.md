*Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin, Sorcerer)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a pair of platinum rings worth 50+ GP each, which you and the target must wear for the duration)
**Duration:** 10 minutes

You create a metaphysical connection between you and a willing creature you can see until the spell ends. As long as you and the target are on the same plane of existence, each time you or the target take damage, the damage amount (after any [[Resistance|Resistances]] are applied) is divided equally between you and the target (rounded down). If you or the target is healed, the healed amount is divided equally between you and the target (rounded up).

Additionally, if you or the target have the [[Blinded]], [[Deafened]], or [[Poisoned]] condition, both you and the target have that condition. If the condition can be ended by succeeding on a saving throw, both you and the target can attempt the saving throw on your respective turns. If the condition ends for you or the target by any means, it ends for both of you simultaneously.

The spell ends if you or the target drop to 0 [[Hit Points]]. It also ends if the spell is cast again on either of the connected creatures. Either you or the target can end the spell early as a free action on their turn.
