*Level 2 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a pair of platinum rings worth 50+ GP each, which you and the target must wear for the duration)
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch another creature that is willing and create a mystic connection between you and the target until the spell ends. While the target is within 90 feet of you, you each gain a +1 bonus to [[Evasion Class|EC]], [[Armor Class|AC]], and saving throws, and it has [[Resistance]] to all damage. Also, each time it takes damage, you take the same amount of damage. If the target has 0 [[Hit Points]], any damage it takes is fully transferred to you.

The spell ends if you drop to 0 [[Hit Points]] or if you and the target become separated by more than 90 feet. It also ends if the spell is cast again on either of the connected creatures.
