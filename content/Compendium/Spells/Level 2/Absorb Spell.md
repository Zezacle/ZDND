*Level 2 Abjuration (Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take upon being damaged by a spell
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A barrier of magical energy appears and protects you. The barrier absorbs 1d10 + your spellcasting ability modifier worth of damage from the spell. If you reduce the spells damage to 0 with this, you gain [[Advantage]] on the first spell attack you make next turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the shield increases by 1d10 damage for each level above 2nd.
