*Level 2 Evocation (Paladin)*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 5 feet
**Components:** S, M (metal ring worth 10+ GP)
**Duration:** 1 minute

Centered on a point you can see within range, create a ring of fire 5 feet tall with a radius of 15 feet. This fire is magical and does not ignite objects. You are immune to this fire and can end the effect at will.

Upon casting, any creatures caught in the spaces of the fire must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 3d8 damage and you choose which side of the ring of fire they are forced to. On a success, they take half as much damage and they choose which side of the ring of fire they are forced to.

On following turns, any creature that starts it's turn in the fire or moves through the ring's flaming edge must repeat the saving throw and take the appropriate damage. If you are conscious, you can allow a creature to automatically succeed on this saving throw.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Damage increases 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.
