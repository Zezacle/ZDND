_Level 2 Abjuration (Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Component:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You tap into your life force to heal yourself. Expend one or two of your [[Hit Point Dice]] to heal an amount equal to the maximum roll of the expended dice.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The number of unexpended Hit Dice you can expend increases by one for each spell slot level above 2.
