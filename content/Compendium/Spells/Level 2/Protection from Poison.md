*Level 2 Abjuration (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Poisoned]] condition, and it has [[Resistance]] to Poison damage.

***If Using Potion Toxicity Rules:*** *A creature under the effects of [[Protection from Poison]] automatically succeeds on their saves to take less Toxicity when drinking a potion.*
