*Level 2 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Paladin, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[Ritual]]
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 10 minutes

You touch a willing creature, magically linking to them. Until the spell ends, linked creatures have [[Resistance]] to any damage dealt by a creature linked to them. Additionally, no creatures linked by the spell can reduce any other linked creature's [[Hit Points]] below half of their maximum HP while the spell is active.

If a linked creature deals damage to a non-linked creature, their link is broken, and they no longer benefit from the protections granted by the spell. Any creature affected by the spell can waive their own protections granted by the spell at will.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional willing creature per spell slot higher than 2nd.
