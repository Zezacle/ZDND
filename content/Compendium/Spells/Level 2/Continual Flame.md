*Level 2 Evocation (Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (ruby dust worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Until dispelled

A flame springs from an object that you touch. The effect casts [[Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. It looks like a white flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched.
