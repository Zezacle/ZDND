*Level 1 Evocation (Paladin)*

**Casting Time:** Action or [[Ritual]]
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

For the duration of the spell, each time you hit a target with a Melee weapon or an [[Unarmed Strike]], you can cast [[Divine Smite]] at Level 1 expending the requisite spell slot, but without using a Bonus Action.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Level at which you can cast [[Divine Smite]] increases for each spell slot level above 1.
