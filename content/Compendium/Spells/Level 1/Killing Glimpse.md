*Level 1 Divination (Bard, Paladin, Ranger, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S, M (a weapon)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a weapon and see a brief vision of the last killing blow it was used to inflict. From the vision you can glean the species and race of the killed creature as well as the nature of the strike that killed it, whether that be crushing blow, stealthy backstab, channeled magic, etc.

This vision does not reveal anything about the identity of the wielder of the weapon, nor does it explicitly reveal the victim's identity.

If the spell is used on a weapon that has not killed anything or has been thoroughly cleaned since it's last kill, the spell fails and the spell slot is not used.
