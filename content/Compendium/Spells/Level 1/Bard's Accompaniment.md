*Level 1 Illusion (Bard)*

**Casting Time:** Action or [[Ritual]]
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

Conjure a group of four or five ghostly beings. These can take any color or general shape the caster chooses. The will provide any backup vocals or instrumentals the bard needs for their performance. For the duration of the spell all [[Performance]] checks have [[Advantage]].
