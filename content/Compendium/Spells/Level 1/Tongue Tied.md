*Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Sight
**Components:** S, M (a bit of string)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You attempt to confuse a targets speech. Target must make a Wisdom saving throw, on a fail the target's speech becomes unintelligible and they cannot cast spells that require a Verbal component for the duration.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The spell's duration increases by 1 minute for each spell slot level above 1.
