*Level 4 Evocation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a chunk of quartz or other crystal worth 25 GP+, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** 1 hour

Form 4 crystal shards that float and encircle your head. As a Bonus Action, you can consume one of the crystal shards to make a ranged spell attack against a target you can see within 90 feet. On a hit, the target takes 4d6 piercing damage. 

Unused shards drop to the ground and shatter like glass when the spell's duration ends.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The number of crystal shards created increases by 1 for each spell slot level above 4.
