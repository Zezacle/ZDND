*Level 4 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Warlock)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Spend an action to concentrate and trap spiraling wind energy in a ball in your palm, ready to release on a following turn. The next unarmed melee strike you hit deals an additional 4d8 force damage. The target must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10 feet. If the target is pushed into another creature or object, it takes an additional 2d8 bludgeoning damage.

If you have an active [[Shadow Clone]] with 5-feet of you, you can command it with a Bonus Action to use the [[Help]] action allowing you to cast this spell without requiring [[Concentration]] and without using your action.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Knockback distance and damage increases by 10 feet and 1d8 damage for each spell slot level above 4.
