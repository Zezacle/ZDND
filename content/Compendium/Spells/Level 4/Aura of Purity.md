_Level 4 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[Resistance]] to Poison damage and [[Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end effects that include the [[Blinded]], [[Charmed]], [[Dazed]], [[Deafened]], [[Frightened]], [[Paralyzed]], [[Poisoned]], or [[Stunned]] condition.
