*Level 3 Abjuration (?)*

**Casting Time:** Action or [[Ritual]]
**Range:** 15 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 10 minutes

Create a magical field that expands up to a 15-foot diameter [[Sphere]] to fill the available space (won't pass through walls or floors). Any sound outside the field cannot be heard inside the field. Any sound made inside the field cannot be heard outside the field, unless it is transceived by magical means akin to [[Sending]]. Creatures and objects can pass through the Dome of Silence, as well as magical effects, provided they are not sound, such as the effects of Thunder damage spells.

As a Bonus Action on each of your turns, you can move the origin point of the spell up to 15 feet.
