*Level 4 Conjuration (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

You touch the surface of a tangible solid barrier such as a wall, floor or ceiling, and attempt to open a portal through it. If there is an open space for a Medium sized creature on the other side of the solid barrier within 30 feet, an exit portal will appear and link with the entry portal creating a [[Void Passage]]. 

The Void Passage appears as an opaque dark purple rift on the surface of the barrier. Any Medium or smaller creature or object that touches a Void Passage is instantly teleported to the other side. The portal closes when the spell ends or if you lose [[Concentration]].

If the surface is too thick for a Void Passage to be established, the spell fails and the spell slot used to cast it is not lost. A Void Passage cannot be established if any amount of lead is between where the entry and exit portals are attempting to be placed.
