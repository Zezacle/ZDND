# Billie the Saboteur
*Medium Humanoid (Halfling), Chaotic Good*

**EC:** 14 - **AC:** 16 ([[Leather Armor]] + [[Shortsword]])
**HP:** 27
**Speed:** 25 ft.
**Initiative:** +3

**Skills:** [[Insight]] +4, [[Perception]] (Expert) +6, [[Sleight of Hand]] +5, [[Stealth]] (Expert) +7
**Senses:** [[Passive Perception]] 16
**Languages:** Common, Undercommon

****

### Traits

***Sneak Attack.*** Billie deals an extra 2d6 damage on attacks they have [[Advantage]] on or against creatures that an ally is within 5 ft. of.

***Halfling Nimbleness.*** Billie can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than them, but them can't stop in the same space.

### Actions

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* 1d6 + 3 piercing damage.

***Swordbreaker.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* 1d4 + 3 piercing damage.
- Disarming: Billie have [[Advantage]] on rolls made to [[Disarm]] a target.

***Throwing Knife. (3x)*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, range 20/60 ft.
- *Hit:* 1d4 + 3 piercing damage.

### Bonus Actions

***Cunning Action.*** Billie takes one of the following actions: [[Dash]], [[Disengage]], or [[Hide]].

***Steady Aim.*** Billie gains [[Advantage]] on the next attack roll they make on their current turn. They cannot use any movement before or after taking this Bonus Action.

***Dual Wielding.*** Billie makes an attack. It cannot be with a weapon they've already attacked with.

### Reactions

***Swordbreak.*** When a melee weapon attack against Billie misses, Billie can use their Reaction to make a [[Disarm]] check against the creature's weapon.
