Last time on Charter of the North

Our intrepid adventurers began their day by obliterating a foe with Atesh casting his first Fireball, followed by a rush to put out the fire before it burned down the East Docks. Inside they found some useful implements, and a lead on Stillwater's local gem dealer, Murdoc.

Following such lead into the Market, they racked their brains until discovering contracts and a key in Main Office which granted them access to Murdoc's storage room in the flooded basement. Upon slaying the great tentacled beast of the dark (and discovering it to be eels) they looted Murdoc's Gem Store and set course back to Toki, to learn the ancient, practiced art of Tattooing.
