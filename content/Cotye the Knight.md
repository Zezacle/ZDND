# Cotye the Knight
*Medium Humanoid (Fiendling), Lawful Good*

**EC:** 12 - **AC:** 19 ([[Splint Armor]] + [[Arm Shield]])
**HP:** 32
**Speed:** 30 ft.
**Initiative:** +1

**Senses:** [[Darkvision]] 30 ft., [[Passive Perception]] 10
**Languages:** Common

****

### Traits
***Paladin Spellcasting.*** Cotye can cast Paladin Spells using Spell Slots.
- Level 1 Slots: 3
	- [[Bane]]: Concentration (1min), CHR Save, on failure, -1d4 to Atk rolls / Saves
	- [[Bless]]: Concentration (1min), grant +1d4 to Atk rolls / Saves to up to 3 allies
	- [[Swift Smite]]: Concentration (1min), Divine Smite doesn't cost a Bonus Action.
	- [[Wardaway]]: Ranged Spell Attack (+4 to hit): CON Save or 2d4 Force + Speed halved

### Actions

***War Pick (Blunt).*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* 1d8 + 3 bludgeoning damage.

***War Pick (Spike).*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* 1d4 + 3 piercing damage.
- Special: This attack ignores AC bonuses from worn Armor, excluding Shields.

***[[Sacred Flame]].*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, range 60 ft.
- *Hit:* DEX Save or take 1d8 Radiant. No benefit from partial Cover.

***Lay On Hands. (20 pts)*** Spend points to heal a commensurate amount of HP on a creature you Touch.

### Bonus Actions

***[[Divine Smite]].*** On melee hit expend a spell slot to deal an additional 2d8 Radiant damage. Upcasting adds 1d8 / additional slot level.
