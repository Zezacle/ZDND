### Traits

***Frenzy Attack.*** Georgia can choose to gain [[Advantage]] on attack rolls until the start of her next turn. She deals an extra 2d6 damage on the first hit. All attacks against him have [[Advantage]] during this time.

***Danger Sense.** Georgia has [[Advantage]] on Dexterity saving throws.

### Actions

***Dual Hook Swords.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft.
- *Hit:* 2d6 + 3 slashing damage.

***Trip Attack.*** Target must succeed DC 11 STR or DEX saving throw or fall [[Prone]].

### Bonus Actions

***Rage.*** Enter the classic Barbarian's Rage and gain the following effects.
- **Damage Resistance:** [[Resistance]] against Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage.
- **Extra Damage:** +2 damage on hit.
- **Strength Advantage:** [[Advantage]] on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
