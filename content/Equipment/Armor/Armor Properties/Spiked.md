[[Spiked]] armor deals 1d4 piercing damage to any creature that hits you with an [[Unarmed Strike]], Bite attack, or other attack that uses a part of their body to strike.

Additionally, when you [[Grappled|Grapple]] a creature with an [[Unarmed Strike]], you deal 1d4 piercing damage. Each time they start their turn while [[Grappled]] by you, they take an additional 1d4 piercing damage.
