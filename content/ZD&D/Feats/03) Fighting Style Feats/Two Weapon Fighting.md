*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

When you make the extra attack of the [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Light]] property, you can make it as part of the [[Attack]] action instead of as a Bonus Action.

For example, a Fighter with Extra Attack would take the [[Attack]] action, which would permit them to make two attack rolls (one for each weapon) as their first attack and then again for their second attack for four attack rolls total.
