*General Feat (Pre-requisite: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+, Proficiency with a weapon with the [[Ammunition]] property)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Change Target.** If you expend ammunition on a ranged weapon attack and it is countered by some kind of effect such as the [[Shield]] spell and now does no damage, you can use your reaction to expend another ammunition and immediately make another ranged weapon attack on a different target.

**Interchangeable Ammunition.** You can use [[Arrows]] in Crossbows and [[Bolts]] in Bows without [[Disadvantage]].

**Firing Position.** Your ranged attacks while [[Prone]] do not have [[Disadvantage]] within their normal range.
