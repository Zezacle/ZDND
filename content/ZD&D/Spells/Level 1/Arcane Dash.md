*Level 1 Conjuration (Bard, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 20 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Teleport in a straight line up to the end of the spells range. This movement does not provoke [[Opportunity Attacks]]. If you pass within 5ft or through a creature’s space while moving in this way you may make a melee attack against it and add 1d6 force damage on a hit.

For Paladins, this attack cannot use [[Divine Smite]].

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, add 10ft to the range and 1d6 force damage per level above 1st.
