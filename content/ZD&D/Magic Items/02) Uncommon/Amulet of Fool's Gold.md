*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Attunement:** Required

*A gold chain with a chunk of gold ore as an amulet. Or is it?*

Upon attuning to this item, the wearer is granted 1,000gp and can no longer take off the Amulet by any means.

**Secret Curse.** This item is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you have 2,000gp more than when you attuned to it, at which point the Amulet consumes the gold and the curse ends leaving the Amulet inert. If the curse is ended before this by the [[Remove Curse]] spell, the Amulet automatically consumes the nearest 1,000gp and becomes inert.
