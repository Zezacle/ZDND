*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Charges Left:** Recharges at Dawn
> [!checks|no-title clean]
> -
>  - [ ] %% %%

*A scarf made of a nearly weightless translucent blue linen.*

As a reaction to being damaged, you can phase into an intangible form, ignoring the damage and moving up to your maximum speed and passing through creatures and becoming tangible again when you stop. You cannot end your movement in an occupied space.

Upon using this ability, you become vulnerable to Psychic and Force damage until you complete a [[Long Rest]].
