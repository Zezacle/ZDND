### Weapon Properties

| Property       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Ammunition]] | You can use a weapon that has the [[Ammunition]] property to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from it. The type of ammunition required is specified with the weapon's range. Each attack expends one piece of ammunition. Drawing the ammunition is part of the attack (you need a free hand to load a one-handed weapon). After a fight, you can spend 1 minute to recover half the ammunition (round down) you used in the fight; the rest is lost.                                                                                                                                    |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Aquatic]]    | An [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Aquatic]] weapon can make melee attacks in water without [[Disadvantage]].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Defensive]]  | When wielding a weapon with the [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Defensive]] property, as a Reaction to being hit by a melee weapon attack, you may roll your weapon's damage die + your proficiency bonus (if proficient with the weapon) and reduce the damage you receive by the result.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Disarming]]  | While wielding a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Disarming]] weapon, you have [[Advantage]] on rolls made to Disarm a target.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| [[Finesse]]    | When making an attack with a [[Finesse]] weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| [[Heavy]]      | You have [[Disadvantage]] on attack rolls with a [[Heavy]] weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Light]]      | When you take the [[Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Light]] weapon, you can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a different [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Light]] weapon, and you don’t add your ability modifier to the extra attack’s damage unless that modifier is negative. For example, you can attack with a [[Shortsword]] in one hand and a [[Dagger]] in the other using the [[Attack]] action and a Bonus Action, but you don't add your Strength or Dexterity modifier to the damage roll of the Bonus Action unless that modifier is negative. |
| [[Loading]]    | You can fire only one piece of ammunition from a [[Loading]] weapon when you use an action, a Bonus Action, or a Reaction to fire it, regardless of the number of attacks you can normally make.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Long]]       | A [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Long]] weapon adds 10 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for [[Opportunity Attacks]] with it. You have [[Disadvantage]] when attacking targets 5 feet away with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Long]] weapon.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Non-Lethal]] | If an attack with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Non-Lethal]] weapon against a target with two or more HP would reduce the target's [[Hit Points\|HP]] to zero, it instead reduces it to one and renders the target [[Unconscious]].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Quick]]      | When you take the [[Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Quick]] weapon, you can make an extra attack with that weapon at [[Disadvantage]] as a Bonus Action.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| [[Range]]      | A [[Range]] weapon has a range in parentheses after the [[Ammunition]] or [[Thrown]] property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon’s normal range in feet, and the second is the weapon’s long range. When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have [[Disadvantage]] on the attack roll. You can't attack a target beyond the long range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| [[Reach]]      | A [[Reach]] weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for [[Opportunity Attacks]] with it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Siege]]      | A [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Siege]] weapon deals double damage on inanimate objects. Additionally, [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Siege]] weapons deal +2 damage to constructs made of non-organic material.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Sluggish]]   | Each time you attack with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Sluggish]] weapon, it reduces your speed by 5 feet. You can still attack with this weapon when your speed is 0.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| [[Thrown]]     | If a weapon has the [[Thrown]] property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack, and you can draw that weapon as part of the attack. If the weapon is a Melee weapon, use the same ability modifier for the attack and damage rolls that you use for a melee attack with that weapon.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Tripping]]   | While wielding a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Tripping]] weapon, you can make a melee attack roll against the target who must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity saving throw or fall [[Prone]] instead of taking damage. The DC for the saving throw is 8 + your Strength modifier + you Proficiency Bonus.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| [[Two-Handed]] | A [[Two-Handed]] weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Unseen]]     | Missed ranged attacks with an [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Unseen]] weapon does not reveal your position if you are hidden from the target.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| [[Versatile]]  | A [[Versatile]] weapon can be used to strike in two main ways (usually One-Handed vs [[Two-Handed]]), changing its properties and/or its damage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Vicious]]    | When you hit a creature with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Vicious]] weapon, as a Bonus Action you can make a Charisma ([[Intimidation]]) check against the target. On a fail, they become [[Frightened]] of you until the end of your next turn. If the attack is a [[Critical Hit]], they have [[Disadvantage]] on their saving throw.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
### Weapon Masteries

| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
