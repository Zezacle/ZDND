When you make an extra attack with an [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Mastery/Offhand]] weapon as a result of using the weapon's that has the [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Light]] property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren’t already adding it to the damage.
