When you hit a creature with a [[ZD&D/Glossary/Properties/Weapon Properties/Vicious]] weapon, as a Bonus Action you can make a Charisma ([[Intimidation]]) check against the target. On a fail, they become [[Frightened]] of you until the end of your next turn. If the attack is a [[Critical Hit]], they have [[Disadvantage]] on their saving throw.
