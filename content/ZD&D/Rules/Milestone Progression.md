# Milestone progression

To level up, characters must accumulate a certain number of "Milestones" and possibly reach a specific prerequisite. Milestones are essentially XP chunks, where as XP might be awarded for killing a monster, a Milestone is awarded for clearing its dungeon, or killing a boss, completing a quest, etc.
