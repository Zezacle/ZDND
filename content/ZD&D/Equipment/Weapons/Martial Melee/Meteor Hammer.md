#### [[Meteor Hammer]] (15 GP)
*Martial Melee, 15 GP, 2 lb.*

**Damage:** 1d6 bludgeoning
**Mastery:** [[Topple]]
**Properties:**
- *Special:* 
	- **Tethered.** This weapon is not lost when [[Thrown]]. 
	- **Meteor's Maelstrom.** As an Action, you can spin the Meteor Hammer around creating a 5ft [[Emanation]]. Until the start of your next turn, any creature that enters or is in the [[Emanation]] other than you must make a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d10 bludgeoning damage. If the creature is an Ally, they have [[Advantage]] on their saving throw. While the effect is active, your movement speed is reduced 5ft. The effect ends early if a creature or object takes damage from this effect, you use a Bonus Action or Reaction, if you take damage, or choose to end the effect early.
- [[Finesse]]
- [[Quick]]
- [[Thrown]] (10)
- [[Two-Handed]]
