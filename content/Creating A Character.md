This page assumes you've made a 5e (2014/2024) character before. If you haven't talk to your party/DM for help or watch a YouTube video. All the basics apply, you just use this site instead of a book.

## Step 1: Your Character Sheet

Download the [D&D 2024 Character Sheet.pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kZzLRSx1BQmef22A-ZqFWQswukM5VBWx/view?usp=drive_link) from my Google Drive.

Open it with a PDF Editor of your choice:
- [PDF Gear](https://www.pdfgear.com/) (Recommended, Free)
- Adobe Acrobat
- Mozilla Firefox browser
- Google Chrome browser

> If you wish to Print out your sheet and use pen and paper, please make a .PDF as well and update it every now and then so that your DM can have a copy.

## Step 4: Pick a Background

A background influences your character's history as well as their skills. Choose carefully.

Be sure to add the Equipment that your background grants.

## Step 5: Determine Your Ability Scores

#### Generation Method
In this house we use **Standard Array** or **Point Buy** to determine Ability Scores.
- **Standard Array:** 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8
- **Point Buy:** Use [this calculator](https://redcap.press/character-creation?method=Point+Buy) to determine your scores. You can use the "Custom" Background option to apply your Racial Bonuses.

#### Required Dump Stat
One of your stats must be at most 9 (-1). You'll take longer to reach god status and there might be some funny moments along the way. Big props if you have a -2 modifier.
