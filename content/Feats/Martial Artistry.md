#### [[Martial Artistry]]
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Monk, Rogue or Fighter class)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Combat Artist.** You gain proficiency with [[Baton|Batons]], [[Bola|Bolas]], [[Staff|Staffs]], [[Meteor Hammer|Meteor Hammers]], [[Nunchuks]], [[Rope Dart|Rope Darts]], [[ZD&D/Equipment/Weapons/Simple Ranged/Sling|Slings]], and [[Throwing Knife|Throwing Knives]]. If you were already proficient with the weapon, you learn its [[Weapon Mastery]] property.

When you hit a target with any of these weapons, you can add your Strength ***and*** Dexterity modifiers to the damage roll instead of the ability modifier that would normally be used.
