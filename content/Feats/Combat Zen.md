#### [[Combat Zen]]
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Wise Strikes.** You may use your Wisdom modifier in place of Strength or Dexterity when making attack and damage rolls with weapons you are proficient with.

**Calm in Calamity.** You have [[Advantage]] on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[Frightened]] condition.
