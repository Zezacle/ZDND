#### [[Pacifier]]
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Non-Lethal Master.** You gain a +1 to attack rolls made with weapons that have the [[Non-Lethal]] property. 

**Choke Out.** When you have only one creature [[Grappled]], as an Action you can make a Strength check contested by their Constitution saving throw. On a success, you render the target [[Unconscious]] for 1d4 minutes or until they take damage. You cannot use this ability on a creature larger than you.
