#### [[Flash Recall]]
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Spellcasting feature from a class that prepares spells)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Flash Recall.** As a bonus action, you prepare a spell of 1st level or higher from your spellbook (if you're a wizard) or from your class spell list (if you're not a wizard). This spell must be of a level for which you have spell slots, and it replaces another spell of an equal or higher level that you had previously prepared.

Once you use this feat to recall a spell, you can't do so again until you complete a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]].
