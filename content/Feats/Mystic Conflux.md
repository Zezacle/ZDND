#### [[Mystic Conflux]]
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Attunement Slots.** You have one additional [[Attunement]] slot.

**Identify.** You can cast the [[Identify]] spell without expending a spell slot or material components. You must finish a [[Long Rest]] before you can do so again.
