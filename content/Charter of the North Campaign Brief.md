The King has given his most respected military captain the title of Great Baron and the charter to explore the vast wilderness at the northern edge of his kingdom and claim it in the King's name.

Regular calls to duty have echoed throughout the Kingdom asking for capable persons to join the Great Baron in his frontier town of Windscape, to aid him in his charter. They promise adventure, untold riches, glory and land grants to any intrepid settlers or mercenaries willing to brave these hostile lands.

You are among the first to answer this call. Making the best of a mild winter, you've set course for Windscape, having to exhaust much of your savings to make the trek. But you know this: There is glory, fortune and secrets of the old world in the North, and it will all be worth it if you can play your cards right.

### Characters
*There won't be a Session 0, instead, feel free to discuss your character in Discord!*

You character has traveled from the Kingdom or further to Windscape over the course of this past winter. You either walked the long road in a caravan of other adventurers and settlers or caught a ride on **The Flying Whale**, a merchant ship that is now anchored in the bay.

You've been in town for a day or two before a man named Roald brings an offer. A bounty has been posted. Find and Kill some vermin. He's got an idea where they are, and is willing to split the bounty evenly with anyone willing to help him.

#### Create Your Characters
Use the [[00) Creating a Character|Create a Character]] resource. It will walk you through the whole process using this Homebrew.
- No Noble background
- You get all equipment from Class/Background EXCEPT weapons, armor, shields and gold

*Building your ideal kit will be part of early-level progression. You have **31 GP** to spend on any of the items listed below (see "Available Equipment" section). Your remaining GP is your starting money.*

#### Read Important New Rules
For this campaign we'll be using the version of D&D 2024 on this website, you can treat it as your Wiki. There are two major rule changes that every player will be affected by and should be aware of:
- [[Evasion Class]] or "EC" - A new defensive stat in addition to [[Armor Class]].
- [[Excursion Resting]] - A new resting ruleset to balance out not having 6-8 encounters / day
