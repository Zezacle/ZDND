*[[Longsword]], Very Rare*

**Attunement:** Automatic, no slot consumed
**Cost:** -
**Weight:** 3 lb.
**Uses:** -

*A curved blade with a single honed edge. Crafted by Dwarven Mastersmith Vadanya in the image of blades of past. Cursed by a Demon of Muspelheim, this blade evokes its a cobalt blue fire bearing the beast's rage.*

**Cursed Pact Weapon.** This blade is tethered to the soul of bearer through forces beyond the Material Plane. The bearer cannot change their Pact weapon to any other but this one. Additionally, this Pact Weapon cannot deal Radiant damage.

**Demon's Spite.** When you use a Pact Magic spell slot to cast Eldritch Smite with this weapon, you can change the damage type to Fire damage and roll an additional damage die. You take Fire damage equal to the additional die's roll.

**Masterwork.** You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this [[+1 Weapon]].
