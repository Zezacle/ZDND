A mysterious journal written in a calligraphic form of Common. Only the first quarter is filled out. There are a number of entries interspersed with doodles, arcane notes, reminders, travel plans and more.

#### Entry 1
New Journal, same journey. I think I'll have to be more conservative with my entries in this one, enchanting another may be out of reach for the next few months. 

I met some locals today. Traded the last of the salts I had on hand for some quality venison cuts and jerkied goat. Otherwise not much to report. I should reach the foothills by tomorrow if my map is accurate.

#### Entry 2
So I've run into a problem. Hobgoblins. A wretched scouting party of them. I was lucky to catch sight of them far before they saw me, but if they're in the area I must be more careful. I want to cook this venison tonight before it starts to sour, so I suppose I'll have to create some distance. I don't want my fire to give me away.

#### Entry 3
By the gods above there's more of them. At the end of the Foothills there is a large valley and a smaller mountain range beyond. It seems they've made permanent camp in the valley bed, and its all open. Hardly any cover but grass for miles. It looks like whatever few trees are here, they're felling them for construction material. 

I know there is a coastal swamp somewhere west. Seems the safest bet considering the damn mongrels keep travelling in the east.

#### Entry 4
I crossed the valley north of the Hobgoblins and found out why the bastards needed so much wood, they're building watchtowers. I'll have to remember to give them a wide berth should I trek this area again. 

I'm on the edge of this grand swamp. Curiously, its surrounded in a border of hills. I don't know how such land would come to be, but I'm no geologist, so...

I still haven't spotted a tower akin to the one the Wizard is supposed to be in. I hope he knows where it is. I've spent 2 years now searching for even a hint, and yet--It remains a mystery.

#### Entry 5
I think I was almost eaten this morn. Turns out there is Lizardfolk in them swamps. They ambushed me, but I set a willow ablaze and scared them off. Thankfully, primitives rarely like the sight of magic.

There looks to be a river valley that cuts through these mountains to my south. I think I will follow it and see where it takes me. It's been a hot summer. Well, I suppose hot to me. I could use the slight chill of elevation.

#### Entry 6
I made it through the mountain pass. There was a giant cave mouth on one of the mountain faces. I fear whatever may be inside, and I really hope its not the University, but luckily it seemed rather natural in form.

I left it be, no need to tempt fate. There seems to some kind of tower in the treeline below. I don't think it is what I'm looking for, but I will explore it in the morning nevertheless.

#### Entry 7
Fucking Kobolds.

#### Entry 7.1
I'm still bleeding, even though I applied some healing magic to my wound. I think the little fuck who stabbed me had an accursed blade. Might have to find somewhere to bunker down until I am more well.

#### Entry 8
I can't catch a break. While resting, a Goblin stole my pack. I chased the little green fiend to the mouth of a cave and turned him to dust. I grabbed my pack when suddenly about half dozen of the creatures came stampeding out of the cave with blades drawn. I pushed them down with a Gust of Wind, and disappeared into the trees. Thankfully, it seems I've lost them.

I now sit in some stone ruins. It seems whatever these used to be they were built before the Collapse.

#### Entry 8.1
I found a cellar that led to some kind of alchemy lab. I think this is where I will rest. Sadly, I'm running low on provisions. I think tomorrow I will see what I can scrounge up.

#### Entry 9
There's another swamp to my south. I managed to spear some fish and find some Turgill Roots. It'll make a fine few meals. My wound is finally healed, but my chest still feels heavy. I think I will have to stay in these woods a few more days.

There's also this damned rat that's gotten into my provisions twice now. Next time I see him, I'm gonna club him with my spear.

#### Entry 9.1
Got the pest. Although, now I do feel sorry for the creature.

#### Entry 10
HE'S BACK. I DON'T KNOW HOW BUT HE CAME BACK TO LIFE.

I'm nearly certain its the same rat too. He's got the same mottling and one of his legs is busted. And its not as if he survived my blow, he was certainly dead after I struck him.

I came back from searching the northern edge of the forest and I think I found the Wizard's Tower too! By the gods, today is full of surprises.

#### Entry 10.1
I know why he lives! I had dropped the creature in the back room because I didn't want him rotting in my vicinity, and there's the most peculiar thing here. A liquid is seeping through the stone floor and my divining tells me it has necromantic properties! It brought the rat back!

This will be most curious to study, but tomorrow I have to check out the tower.

Also, I've named this rat Baldr, since he refused to die. In the morning I'm going to build a simple trap to catch him. I don't know how hard it is to tame an undead rodent, but it seems an interesting endeavor.

#### Entry 11
I'm quite certain this tower is the one I was searching for, but its not what I hoped. I think the Wizard is long dead. There is much of interest here, but all of it is in the upper levels sealed by some kind of magic.

Using my own, I managed to break into the second level, and found a most peculiar thing-- An peculiar orb with profound divination energy, seeming of Jotun origin by the Giant runes engraved in it.

Frankly, it's painful to carry, it makes the nerves in my hand numb.

There's lots more, I decided to detail it in my other notes as not to clutter this journal up like my previous one.

Also, I caught Baldr! He's rather upset with me, but I've left him some food and water in his crate. It's lot easier to feed an angry animal with a mage hand than your actual hands. He's kind of mindless in a lot of ways. I hope his little mind was not corrupted too much. I know there are ways to charm a beast, if I imbued such a spell into an item he could wear, perhaps that would be the best way to calm his wrath.

#### Entry 12
I went back to the tower today. Sadly I made little progress entering the higher floors. I think the protective magic has some sort of power source. There's quite a distinct path leading to a underground entrance in a nearby hill. I think I will explore it tomorrow.

#### Entry 13
Fuck. I went into the underground today... I think whatever power source the tower uses is down there, but-- I couldn't go in. There's a golem protecting it.

Not again. I'm stronger now. I might could defeat it with my spells, but-- I just got over the nightmares. There's no one here to pull me out if things go sideways. Not like last time.

The tower can wait.

#### Entry 14
Baldr seems to be coming around. Luckily it seems in his now undead form, he needn't food at all. I still haven't let him loose yet, but I've been getting him accustomed to touch with my mage hand. He likes to bite it.

I went back to the southern swamp today and saw a Keep. Right on the coast. It looks completely abandoned, but in quite good shape. Also, there was a Turtlefolk asleep, floating in the swamp's waters. I've known their kind to be pleasant, so I might make introductions soon. Maybe they can tell me more about this area. Hopefully this one speaks a dialect of Beast Tongue that I understand.

#### Entry 15
This Keep is magnificent! Somehow its managed to mostly escape the ravages of time. Its still overgrown and there's plenty of holes in the structure, but it's quite well preserved. I write this while I sit in a library. It seems mice have gotten the majority of the reading material but there's a peculiar glass case. It's getting dark from storm clouds. I think I'll just stay the night here. The fire I've started in this hearth has warmed things up nicely.

Baldr should be fine on his own. Although I worry he'll have found a way out by the time I return.

#### Entry 15.1
Every book in this case is pristine! All of them from before the collapse! Before Ragnarök even! There's an incredible wealth of knowledge here.

#### Entry 16
I spent an entire extra day in the Keep's tower reading. There's so much here, but its harder to decipher than Old Elvish. The dialect of the old world is so different. Its hard to imagine the world split into distinct realms. It seems the earth I walk now was once Midgard before the Collapse. This Keep was the home of some lesser noble and his family. I want to write more, but I'd rather keep reading!

#### Entry 16.1
I've found it! Or, I should say, I've found proof that its *real*. Too long I've been searching through fogged memory of those born before the Collapse, but it the clue was here all along! On the South Western coast! I found a book describing the various factions and it spoke of the University! Its REAL! I was right to believe all along!

It says its up the coast in the shadow of the mountains, but I know how much the landscape has changed during the Collapse. I just hope it isn't buried.

I'm leaving the Keep now even though the sun in setting soon. I'm going to pick up Baldr and my things and set off. University of ancient knowledge, here I come!

#### Entry 16.2
I'm back in the lab. Baldr's still here and I've brought the books. While I was descending the Keep's towers I saw some well-armed horse riders to the south. By the time I made it to the treeline, they'd crossed the creek. I think they probably saw me.

I don't think they will find me down here, so I'm going to rest for the night and set out in the morning. My hands are shaking, from excitement. I don't even know if I'll be able to rest. I might just go back to reading. Even if I wake late in the day, I'll just stop in the base of the tower and rest there for the night. Oh, I just remembered the golem. Surely, he'd have no reason to leave his post, yes?

Now I'm definitely not going to be able to sleep. Luckily Baldr will share in my insomnia and keep me company.

*(The remaining journal pages are blank.)*
