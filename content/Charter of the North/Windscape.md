## NPCs Met So Far:

**Great Baron of the Northern Wild**
- Male Dwarf, middle aged (for a dwarf), Dark Red wavy hair, Peg Leg, carries a cane.

**Ulra Keatison, Right Hand of the North**
- Male Human, early 40s, Tall bald man with short well-groomed black beard.

**Goji, Sailing Merchant and Owner of the Three Whale Fleet**
- Male Halfling, early 40s, well dressed in navy blues and beiges with perfectly trimmed short beard
	- Sailing merchant of Artifacts and Exotic goods. Made port in Windscape last year and fell in love with the town. Has great expectations of the town's potential for business.
	- Heavily invested in the local tavern influencing a name change to The Well-Oiled Whale, provides all their beverage importing.
