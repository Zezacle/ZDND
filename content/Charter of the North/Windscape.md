## NPCs Met So Far:

**Great Baron of the Northern Wild**
- Male Dwarf, middle aged (for a dwarf), Dark Red wavy hair, Peg Leg, carries a cane.

**Ulra Keatison, Right Hand of the North**
- Male Human, early 40s, Tall bald man with short well-groomed black beard.

**Goji, Sailing Merchant and Owner of the Three Whale Fleet**
- Male Halfling, early 40s, well dressed in navy blues and beiges with perfectly trimmed short beard
	- Sailing merchant of Artifacts and Exotic goods. Made port in Windscape last year and fell in love with the town. Has great expectations of the town's potential for business.
	- Heavily invested in the local tavern influencing a name change to The Well-Oiled Whale, provides all their beverage importing.

**Lickle, the Brass-Scaled Blacksmith**
- Male Dragonborn, ?? years old, Brass-scaled dragonborn. Often seen in smithy's apron covered in soot
	- Gruff in attitude and overworked.

**Melshi, the Whitesmith**
- Male Halfling, mid 20s, short brown hair and missing a tooth
	- Came to Windscape seeking opportunity to build a business, quickly redirected efforts to becoming a blacksmith apprentice when he met Lickle

**Vanya, the Healer**
- Non-binary Fae Elf, young adult (for a fae elf), Long silky hair of fair color. Often dressed in white robes with gold and green accents
	- Wise and cryptic healer and faith leader. Lives out of the Temple and welcomes all faiths, from Aesir, Vanir and even the Dvergarian Grand Patrons.
	- Enchants and sells (at cost) Totems of Sanctuary for adventurers to keep them safe on their journeys.
