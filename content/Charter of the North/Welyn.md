### Traits

**Agile Movement.** [[Opportunity Attacks]] have [[Disadvantage]] against Welyn.

**Cautious.** Whenever Welyn makes a [[D20 Test]], he can instead roll 2d10.

**Halfling Nimbleness.** Welyn can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than him, but he can't stop in the same space.

**Good Help.** Whenever Welyn takes the [[Help]] action, he grants an additional 1d4 bonus to the [[D20 Test]].

**Naturally Stealthy.** Welyn can take the [[Hide|Hide]] action even when he is obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than him.

**Quick Study.** Welyn learns new [[Skills]] and Proficiencies faster than most.

### Actions

**Dagger.** Melee Attack Roll: `dice: 1d20 +4` (+4) to hit, reach 5 ft.
- Hit: `dice: 1d4 +2` (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage. (Avg. 4)

### Bonus Action

**Quick Dagger Attack.** Welyn makes a [[Dagger]] attack at [[Disadvantage]] using the [[Quick]] property.
