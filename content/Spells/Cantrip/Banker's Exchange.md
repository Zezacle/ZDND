#### [[Banker's Exchange]]
*Transmutation Cantrip (Artificer, Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

With a wave of your hand you can convert up to 100 GP worth of currency of one denomination to an equivalent of a greater or lesser denomination. 

This spell cannot convert one currency to another.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** When casted at 1st level, you can convert up to 500 GP worth of non-magical gemstones such as Rubies, Diamonds, Sapphires, etc. to their average market value in a currency of your choice. You must have seen and held the type of currency at least once before to generate it.

When casted at 2nd level, you can convert up to 1 pound of non-magical valuable made of precious metals or gemstones such as Jewelry, Silverware, or other trinket into its average market value in a currency of your choice. You must have seen and held the type of currency at least once before to generate it.
