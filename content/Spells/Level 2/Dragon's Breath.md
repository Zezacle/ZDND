#### [[Dragon's Breath]]
*Level 2 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self, 30ft Cone
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

As you cup your hands over your mouth and speak the command word, a cone of flames erupts from your mouth. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 3d8 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

The fire ignites any flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.
