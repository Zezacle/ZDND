#### [[Lost and Found]]
*Level 1 Divination (Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, M (1 platinum piece (consumed))
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Summon and pay a pink wisp to lead you to the most valuable item within a 30ft radius. The wisp only leads you to items that have been discarded, lost or otherwise abandoned by the last owner.

This wisp can’t locate an object if any thickness of lead blocks a direct path between you and the object.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Radius increases by 10ft for each spell slot level above 1.
