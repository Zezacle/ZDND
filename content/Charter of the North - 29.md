The tavern cellar is chilled, despite being out of the wind for which the town is named for. Many flickering candles burn on pewter dishes illuminating this makeshift meeting space as pillows and hides make up the seating around a upturned crate that functions as a table in the center. A thin column of smoke drifts out of a tin mug in the center, filling the room with the pleasant aroma of some kind of pine-based incense.

Lana: "Sit, please. Cybil, more wine."

### Hrimtani Dream Sequence

Kestrel, you stand atop a wooden palisade in the bitter cold of an early winter night. Looking out into the frozen forest beyond, you instinctively pull the cloak around your shoulders tighter. Looking down you notice that you wear the uniform of your order. That which protects the frostbitten city of Hrimtani and once protected the Yharnam, before it was taken by the Aesir.

You turn from your post looking back into the city, dread fills you--as you've stood in this very spot before. Your numb hand instinctively reaches for your blade. As you wrap your fingers around the grip, it sears your palm with heat as a bright white flashes over the distant buildings through the snowfall.

{Trigger ALARM!!!! wait a sec, Trigger P1 START!!!}

You begin sprinting, down the steps, through the snow, across the iced footbridge that hangs over a frozen river. Light shines into the cloudy sky as a great blueish white fire erupts on the upper level.

{Trigger SCREAMS!!! Activate Hrimtani Scene}

Screams from the market carry over the wind, somehow not drowned out over the blood pounding in your ears. As you run, another guardsman joins beside you, the wind rips their hood from their head revealing Bronze scales. 

Atesh: "The people in the market! We must save them!"

Two more guardsmen join you on your left. A tall one with horns and a bow. The other, hobbling along with a staff, shield, rounded shell and bright lantern.

Across the bridge in front of you, the doors to the market gates burst open. A man, immolated in blue-ish white flames screams before tumbling over the edge and crunching into the ice below. A flaming blue creature with horns, fangs and tusks steps into the gateway laughing manically.
